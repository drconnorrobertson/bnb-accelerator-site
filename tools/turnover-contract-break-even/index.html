Turnover Contract Break-Even Calculator

AI-assisted editorial guide and tool · Published November 21, 2026

Two cleaning proposals can have different cost structures even when they cover the same work. This calculator compares one monthly retainer plus a per-turn charge against another per-turn charge. Enter the same property, service scope and month for both proposals. The starting values illustrate the arithmetic; they are not market prices.

Enter your assumptions

The starting values are a hypothetical worked example, not your property data. Calculations run in your browser; entries are not submitted or saved after a reload. Use one consistent currency where money is involved.

Costs exclude anything not entered. A fractional crossover is a mathematical point, not a billable job.

Match the scope first

Before entering a rate, compare what the quote covers: linen handling, supply replenishment, inspection, travel and unusual checkout conditions. A lower number for a smaller scope is not a saving on equivalent work. Add known compulsory charges consistently or record them outside the model. The calculator treats the two entered service structures as complete for the selected month.

Read the crossover

Option A equals the monthly retainer plus its per-turn charge multiplied by turnover count. Option B is its per-turn charge multiplied by the same count. Equal cost occurs at retainer divided by the difference between B’s and A’s per-turn rates. If the rates are equal, there is no unique crossover. A negative crossover does not describe an attainable nonnegative workload.

Worked example

With a 300 retainer, 60 per turn for A and 100 per turn for B, ten turns cost 900 under A and 1,000 under B. The numerical crossover is 7.5 turns. Because actual turnovers are whole jobs, compare seven and eight jobs individually before describing a threshold. At eight turns A is 780 and B is 800.

Test a quieter month

Replace the expected count with a plausible quieter month and retain the same quoted rates. At five turns the example changes to 600 for A and 500 for B. This does not determine which contract to sign: minimum terms, cancellation provisions and the provider’s ability to deliver the work remain separate facts. Save the dated proposals alongside the scenarios.
