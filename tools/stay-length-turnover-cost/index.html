Stay-Length Turnover Cost Calculator

AI-assisted editorial guide and tool · Published November 21, 2026

A fixed cleaning or preparation cost creates a different cost per occupied night for a two-night stay and a seven-night stay. This tool spreads one entered turnover cost over two stay lengths and adds your selected variable cost per occupied night. It does not set a nightly price or apply a booking platform’s fee rules.

Enter your assumptions

The starting values are a hypothetical worked example, not your property data. Calculations run in your browser; entries are not submitted or saved after a reload. Use one consistent currency where money is involved.

Only the entered cost categories are modeled. Cost per occupied night is not a recommended selling price.

Define the cost categories

Put costs incurred once per completed stay in the turnover field. Put the selected costs that change with each occupied night in the variable field. A supply item used once per booking should not also be included per night. The model assumes the entered turnover cost is the same for both lengths; if a longer stay changes the cleaning scope, calculate that difference separately.

Read total and nightly cost together

Total cost equals turnover cost plus nightly variable cost multiplied by stay length. Cost per occupied night divides that total by the number of nights. A longer stay usually spreads the fixed component more widely, but its total cost can still be higher. Neither result measures revenue, margin or the cost of nights left empty around the reservation.

Worked example

With a fixed turnover cost of 140 and variable cost of 20 per night, two nights cost 180 in total or 90 per occupied night. Seven nights cost 280 in total or 40 per occupied night. The difference is 50 per occupied night. This is the effect of the selected cost assumptions, not a recommendation for a minimum-stay rule.

Apply the scenario carefully

Compare the result with the actual reservation pattern, staffing arrangement and service scope. If a linen change or mid-stay service occurs only after a certain length, the simple model needs a separate calculation. Keep taxes, debt service and other omitted costs visible elsewhere. Review the platform’s current settings before making any operational change; this tool does not change a listing.
