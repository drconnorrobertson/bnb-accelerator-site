Same-Day Turnover Window Calculator

AI-assisted editorial guide and tool · Published November 20, 2026

This is a same-day capacity screen for similar turnovers within one shared time window. It shows both total team-minutes and the estimated longest team assignment when jobs are divided as evenly as possible. It does not build a route or guarantee readiness. All tasks are assumed sequential within a team, and actual addresses, access delays and differing job sizes still need a schedule.

Enter your assumptions

The starting values are a hypothetical worked example, not your property data. Calculations run in your browser; entries are not submitted or saved after a reload. Use one consistent currency where money is involved.

Capacity screen only. Uniform job times and balanced assignment do not prove route feasibility or guaranteed readiness.

Use the same-day window

Measure from the earliest realistic access time to the required completion time for this group of jobs. Do not use a week’s total working hours. Enter teams that are actually available during this window, not every person on the roster. A team is treated as one work unit with the stated per-job duration.

Read aggregate and team results together

Each job uses cleaning plus inspection minutes. The model adds travel between jobs after the first job assigned to each team. Combined capacity is teams multiplied by the window. The longest balanced assignment uses the ceiling of jobs divided by teams. Negative team headroom warns that the equal-duration jobs do not fit that assignment even if aggregate capacity appears sufficient.

Worked example

Three jobs each need 80 minutes of cleaning and 10 of inspection. Two teams have 180 minutes, and between-job travel takes 20. Modeled labor is 290 team-minutes versus 360 of aggregate capacity. However, one team receives two jobs: 90 plus 20 plus 90 equals 200 minutes. That team is 20 minutes beyond the window despite aggregate spare capacity.

Replace the uniform-job assumption

A larger property, late checkout or separate inspection crew can invalidate equal-duration assumptions. Record actual addresses and earliest access times in a scheduling tool. Add realistic delivery, key and supply tasks to the inputs or schedule rather than hiding them inside apparent slack. This screen cannot establish that an individual contractor can perform work simultaneously at different properties.

Use the result for a dispatch decision

If either aggregate slack or team headroom is negative, revise the plan before promising readiness. Consider authorized staffing, timing or scope changes and recalculate with their actual effects. Even a positive result needs route and task confirmation. Save the version used for dispatch and compare actual durations afterward to improve future estimates without representing the result as a guarantee.
