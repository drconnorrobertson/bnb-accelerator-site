Use the same evidence behind every number

These tools organize your inputs; they do not verify a property, predict a return or replace professional review. Use the same dates and definitions when comparing results, and keep forecasts separate from collected revenue.

  1. Record the source and period for revenue, expenses and available nights.
  2. Use current lender estimates and written vendor quotes in the purchase budget.
  3. Reconcile platform statements with bank deposits before treating a payout as profit.
  4. Keep cash reserves and unresolved assumptions visible in the downside case.
