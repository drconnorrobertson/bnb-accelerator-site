Owner Cash-Allocation Worksheet

AI-assisted editorial guide and tool · Published November 20, 2026

This calculator subtracts amounts you designate from a cash balance and shows the remaining modeled allocation. It does not decide whether guest funds, deposits or other money may legally be distributed. Obtain the relevant agreement and professional guidance first, and enter excluded amounts yourself. The example is a bookkeeping planning exercise, not a recommendation to withdraw funds.

Enter your assumptions

The starting values are a hypothetical worked example, not your property data. Calculations run in your browser; entries are not submitted or saved after a reload. Use one consistent currency where money is involved.

User-designated exclusions and reserve are assumptions. This tool does not determine legal availability or authorize distributions.

Define the starting balance

Use a dated account or ledger balance and identify its scope. Available bank cash and a statement’s ending balance can differ because transactions are pending. Avoid combining money from separate owners or accounts without a reconciled basis. Record the source and date so another reviewer knows exactly which funds were included.

Designate exclusions independently

The excluded-funds input is supplied by you after reviewing applicable obligations. The calculator cannot identify trust money, refundable deposits, taxes, future-stay receipts or contractual restrictions. It does not turn an amount into distributable cash by leaving the field at zero. Keep the basis for each exclusion outside the calculator in the responsible records.

Understand the result

The model subtracts excluded funds, pending bills and the selected reserve from recorded cash. A positive remainder is displayed as the modeled ceiling. A negative remainder displays a zero ceiling and a shortfall. This separates a cash gap from a negative proposed distribution and helps the reviewer see which retained amounts exceed the starting balance.

Worked example

With 3,000 currency units in cash, 500 designated as excluded, 1,000 retained for bills and a 2,000 reserve, the modeled balance is negative 500. The allocation ceiling is zero and the shortfall is 500. If the reserve were reduced, the arithmetic would change, but that would not establish that reducing it is prudent or permitted.

Review before a transfer

Reconcile outstanding payments and verify who may approve an actual distribution. Check whether another owner, lender or agreement imposes restrictions not represented here. Save the assumptions and approval date with any resulting transaction. A numerical ceiling is only as complete as the obligations entered; it is not evidence that an omitted bill or liability disappeared.
