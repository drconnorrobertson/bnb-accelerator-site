Can you buy a home with an ADU for a short-term rental?

Published September 24, 2026·6 min read

Yes, a home with an accessory dwelling unit can be an attractive short-term-rental purchase, but only if the property's specific legal uses and purchase economics support your plan. An ADU is not automatically a second STR, and projected nightly rent from it may not help you qualify for the mortgage. Before bidding, determine whether you will rent the main house, the ADU, or both; obtain address-specific permission for each; and underwrite a fallback if the ADU cannot be rented nightly. The purchase price should reflect rights and income you can verify, not the seller's most optimistic two-listing story.

Define the rental plan and prove the ADU is legal

Ask for permits, certificate of occupancy or equivalent records, approved plans, and the local authority's classification of the additional space. A detached cottage, basement apartment, or garage conversion may be advertised as an ADU without being a lawful dwelling. Confirm the recorded property type, sleeping capacity, parking, access, utilities, and any owner-occupancy or long-term-lease conditions. An unpermitted space should receive no independent rental income in your offer model until its use is resolved.

Ask the municipality about the exact parcel and the exact proposed use of each space. Its ADU ordinance and STR ordinance may impose different requirements. For example, Montgomery County, Maryland's STR FAQ says an ADU cannot operate as a short-term residential rental there. That is a local example, not a national rule. Other jurisdictions may allow a different arrangement, subject to owner-occupancy, license, or guest limits. Check the current local rules and obtain written, address-specific confirmation before valuing an ADU as a nightly rental. Separate mortgage qualification from the investment forecast

An ADU can be part of a one-unit property for some mortgage purposes, but that does not make every ADU revenue stream eligible in every loan program. Fannie Mae's ADU guidance describes program-specific treatment of ADUs and rental income. Its separate subject-property STR-income topic expressly excludes income from an ADU. Do not combine a main-house projected STR statement and an ADU Airbnb estimate and assume the lender will count both. Get the lender's written treatment of the actual property, occupancy, ADU, and documents before waiving a financing contingency.

A seller may call the property 'two units,' while the appraiser or lender treats it as a one-unit home with an ADU. That difference affects comparable sales, loan product, down payment, and accepted rent evidence. Send the lender permits, floor plan, leases, proposed use, and insurance information early. Also ask the insurer to quote the exact combination of owner occupancy, main-house STR use, ADU tenancy, and shared structures. A mortgage approval is not proof that the intended rental operation is lawful or profitable. Compare the lender's answer with the conventional-versus-DSCR decision guide.

Price the ADU using a conservative fallback

Create two separate income and cost ledgers. The main house needs its own comparable STR evidence, seasonality, cleaning, utilities, management, insurance, maintenance, and furnishing costs. The ADU needs its own legally supportable use and income evidence. Include repairs to legalize or maintain it, separate access, parking, fire-safety work, utility allocation, permits, and a reserve. Do not use whole-property STR comparables to validate two separate listings, or use a lease estimate as if it were nightly gross revenue. See the cash-needed-to-buy framework for the all-in basis.

Illustrative only: suppose a buyer offers $520,000 because they believe the ADU adds $50,000 of value through a second STR listing. If the local authority confirms only the main house may operate nightly, that $50,000 premium needs a new basis: verified long-term ADU rent, comparable sale value, or a buyer's documented personal use—not an unavailable Airbnb projection. The numbers are a decision example, not an appraisal, market statistic, or promised return. Stress the main-house STR revenue downward, remove the ADU's nightly income entirely, and recalculate cash flow and cash-on-cash return after the actual financing and insurance quotes. If the investment clears only when both listings operate nightly, pause or reduce the maximum offer.

Compare that revised model with a lawful single-family STR and a lawful duplex at similar total cash invested. A duplex may have different unit, loan, and rental-permission treatment; the duplex purchase guide addresses that separate decision. An ADU may be valuable as a flexible long-term rental or owner space, but only when that use is documented and its incremental cost makes sense.

Set an offer decision and a verification deadline

Proceed when the ADU's lawful status is documented, the local authority confirms the planned rental use for each space, the lender and insurer accept the arrangement, and the conservative model still meets your hurdle. Renegotiate when the seller's price assumes a second STR but the ADU supports only long-term rent or personal use. Delay while a permit, occupancy, appraisal, insurance, or financing question remains unresolved within a usable contingency period. Reject the deal if the ADU is unpermitted and legalization is infeasible, the local use rule defeats the required income, or the fallback cannot carry the purchase.

Before an offer hardens, request the property records, local written use answer, lender-specific ADU treatment, insurer quote, and separate revenue support. Then put the main-house-only and ADU-fallback scenarios next to the seller's claim. Book a call to compare this ADU purchase with other STR candidates. Frequently asked questions

Can I Airbnb an ADU on a property I buy?

It depends on the address, the ADU's legal status, local STR rules, owner-occupancy conditions, and property documents. Obtain a current parcel-specific answer before counting nightly income.

Will a lender count the ADU's projected Airbnb income?

Do not assume so. Fannie Mae's subject-property STR-income topic excludes ADU income. Ask your lender how the actual property and its documented rents will be evaluated.

Should I pay more for a home with an ADU if the ADU cannot be an STR?

Only if its verified long-term rental, personal-use, or comparable-sale value supports the premium after added costs. Remove unavailable nightly income from the purchase model.

Is buying a home with an ADU the same as buying a duplex for an STR?

No. Legal unit classification, STR permission, appraisal, financing, and income evidence may differ. Verify the property's recorded status instead of relying on marketing language.
