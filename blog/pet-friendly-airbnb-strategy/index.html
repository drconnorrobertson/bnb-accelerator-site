Should Your Airbnb Be Pet Friendly?

Published April 28, 2025·6 min read

Pet friendly is one of the highest impact filters in short-term rental search and one of the most commonly declined. The owners who decline it are usually protecting against a risk that is smaller than they think, and the owners who accept it carelessly create the problem they feared.

The case for accepting pets

  • Filter visibility. A meaningful share of travelers filter for pet friendly, and in many markets the competing supply that allows pets is much thinner than total supply.
  • Longer bookings and less price sensitivity. Travelers with pets have fewer alternatives, which shows up in both conversion and rate tolerance.
  • Shoulder season fill. Pet friendly properties frequently convert better in the weeks that are hardest to fill.
  • Pet fees, which offset the incremental cleaning cost when set realistically.

The risks, stated accurately

The common fear is catastrophic damage. The more frequent reality is incremental wear: hair in soft furnishings, scratches on floors and door frames, odor in textiles, and yard cleanup. Those are manageable with the right materials and a realistic fee.

Note also that service animals are treated differently under applicable law and are generally not subject to pet policies or fees. That is worth understanding before writing your rules, and the specifics should be confirmed with counsel or your platform's guidance. See guest screening and damage prevention.

Building a property that handles pets

  1. Hard flooring throughout, with washable rugs rather than wall to wall carpet. This single decision resolves most of the risk.
  2. Performance fabrics on upholstery, and slipcovers that can be laundered.
  3. A fenced yard where possible, which is itself a searchable feature and a strong differentiator.
  4. A dedicated pet station: bowls, towels, a waste bag dispenser, and a designated relief area.
  5. Clear rules posted: where pets may be, whether they can be left alone, and cleanup expectations.

Setting the policy

  • Set a per stay pet fee rather than per night, which reads better to guests and covers the incremental cleaning either way.
  • Limit the number of pets, stated explicitly, so expectations are documented before arrival.
  • Brief the cleaner that this turnover includes pet protocol, and pay accordingly. Cleaners who discover it themselves stop taking your bookings. See turnover systems.
  • Adjust the listing content, because pet friendly guests search for specific things: fencing, flooring, and nearby walking areas.

The practical verdict

For a property with hard flooring, durable furnishings, and preferably a fenced yard, accepting pets is one of the cheapest revenue improvements available. For a property with light colored carpet and delicate upholstery, it is an expensive decision dressed as an easy one.

That means the answer is frequently determined at furnishing time rather than at policy time, which is another argument for deciding the guest profile before you buy the furniture. See how to furnish an Airbnb.

Frequently asked questions

Is it worth making an Airbnb pet friendly?

For a property with hard flooring, durable furnishings, and ideally a fenced yard, it is one of the cheapest revenue improvements available, because competing pet friendly supply is usually much thinner than total supply. For a property with light carpet and delicate upholstery, it is an expensive decision.

How much should I charge as a pet fee?

Set it per stay rather than per night, at a level that realistically covers the incremental cleaning and periodic deep cleaning. A per stay structure reads better to guests and is simpler for cleaners and bookkeeping alike.

What damage do pets actually cause in short-term rentals?

The common fear is catastrophic damage. The frequent reality is incremental wear: hair in soft furnishings, scratches on floors and door frames, odor in textiles, and yard cleanup. Hard flooring, performance fabrics, and washable rugs resolve most of it.

Do pet policies apply to service animals?

Service animals are treated differently under applicable law and are generally not subject to pet policies or fees. Confirm the specifics with counsel or your platform's guidance before writing your rules.
