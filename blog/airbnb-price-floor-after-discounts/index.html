How to check an Airbnb price floor after discounts

Test whether a published room rate falls below your operating floor once a discount is applied.

Worked example

A $160 suggested rate with a 15% discount becomes $136 before fees and taxes. If your room-rate floor is $145, that quote misses the floor by $9 even though the suggested rate clears it.

What to record and verify

Preview a guest quote for the same arrival and departure. Identify which channel applied the discount. If you change the rule, retest a discounted and undiscounted date so you know which setting moved the result.
