Which system controls Airbnb rates and stay rules?

Write down where each setting is edited and where you verify its published value.

Worked example

A pricing tool shows $180 and a three-night minimum, but the channel calendar shows $180 and two nights. Track rate and stay rules as separate settings. Ask which connection owns each value and when it last published.

What to record and verify

Make a row for rate, minimum stay, availability, and promotion. Record the editing system, the publishing system, and the place to check on the channel. Test one date first and verify what a guest sees.
