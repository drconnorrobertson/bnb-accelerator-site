Should you buy an STR property previously removed from Airbnb?

Published September 25, 2026·6 min read

Do not pay an Airbnb-dependent price for an STR property if you cannot establish whether a prior listing removal will affect your ability to publish and receive bookings after closing. A city's permission to host and Airbnb's willingness to carry a listing are separate gates. The seller's account, listing and reservations do not transfer to you, and creating a new listing to evade an enforcement action is not a safe workaround. Ask the seller for authorized evidence of the listing's status and reason, seek a direct platform answer where possible, and underwrite a purchase case without Airbnb revenue until the channel risk is resolved. Then proceed, reprice, delay under protection, or walk away.

Separate property legality from platform eligibility

First verify the parcel's STR permission with the local authority, as in the pre-purchase regulations guide. Then investigate the platform issue independently. A valid permit does not override a private platform decision; an Airbnb listing's removal does not itself prove that the city prohibited the use. Ask the seller whether the prior problem concerned their account, a specific listing, an address match, guest safety, duplicate content, registration, or something else. Do not infer the answer from an unavailable listing link alone.

Airbnb's current host policy says a suspended listing cannot accept new bookings while suspended, and a listing identified for removal is not eligible for reactivation unless an appeal succeeds. Airbnb also says an account cannot be transferred to a new host and reservations cannot be moved to a new host or account. The review-transfer guide covers reputation continuity; this page addresses the separate risk that the buyer's launch channel may be unavailable.

Build an authorized evidence file before the offer deadline

Ask the seller to provide the listing URL and ID, dated screenshots from the listing's issue/status area, relevant Airbnb notices and appeal outcomes, and a written explanation of the event. Have the seller authorize or join a conversation with Airbnb if the platform will discuss the specific record. A prospective buyer may not be entitled to a seller's private account information or a pre-closing guarantee. Record exactly what Airbnb confirms and what remains unanswered; a seller's statement that a new account will work is not platform approval.

Do not ask the seller to hand over login credentials or create a substitute account. Airbnb's circumvention policy prohibits duplicate accounts or listings created to evade enforcement and prohibits facilitating a restricted person's continued access. A genuine change of ownership may be relevant information to present to Airbnb, but the public policy does not promise a new owner's address will be cleared. Avoid advising the buyer to relist first and explain later.

Underwrite a deal that can survive without Airbnb

Separate the seller's historical revenue by channel and do not carry over Airbnb reviews, account status or future reservations as buyer assets. Ask a prospective manager how a new listing would launch on other permitted channels, what fees and lead time apply, and what evidence supports that revenue. The future-reservations guide deals with booking handoff; a platform removal adds a different acquisition risk. Use the downside model to run a zero-Airbnb case until you have a defensible answer.

Illustrative only: if a seller reports $100,000 of annual guest receipts and $70,000 came through Airbnb, do not automatically assign that $70,000 to the buyer. If independently supported alternative channels would replace only $25,000 of that amount during year one, the buyer's illustrative gross-receipts case is $45,000 lower before cost changes. These invented figures are a sensitivity test, not a forecast, market statistic or claim about any property. Recompute cash needed, debt coverage and maximum offer using actual records and vendor quotes. Do not double count reservations that cannot be transferred.

Make the platform question an explicit purchase condition

Have local counsel and the broker define what evidence must arrive by which diligence date and whether the contract protects the buyer if the Airbnb question remains unresolved. A third-party platform may not issue a binding pre-closing assurance, so a contingency cannot manufacture one. If no firm answer is available, the buyer must decide using the supported non-Airbnb case. A city permit, clean title or favorable inspection cannot repair an unsupported booking-channel assumption.

Proceed only when lawful STR use, platform or alternative-channel feasibility, financing and conservative cash flow all work. Renegotiate if the channel mix changes supported value. Delay only under valid contract protection for a specific outstanding response. Reject if the property needs Airbnb bookings to carry its costs and that channel remains unavailable or unverifiable. Frequently asked questions

Can a new owner relist a property Airbnb removed?

Possibly, but do not assume it. Airbnb's public guidance does not promise that a change of ownership clears a listing or address issue. Seek a direct platform answer on the specific case where possible.

Can I buy the seller's Airbnb account and reviews?

No. Airbnb says account ownership and reservations cannot transfer to a new host or account. Model a new-listing ramp rather than paying for account continuity.

Would a city STR permit override an Airbnb removal?

No. Local legal permission and a platform's listing decision are different. Verify both before relying on Airbnb revenue.

What if Airbnb will not answer before closing?

Underwrite the property without assumed Airbnb revenue, test documented alternatives, and use valid contract protection or reject an Airbnb-dependent price.
