How to check guest total when comparing Airbnb pricing tools

Compare the room subtotal and the full quote for the same dates and party size.

Worked example

A $160 room subtotal plus $75 in cleaning is $235 before marketplace fees and taxes. A nearby listing at $180 plus $30 cleaning is $210 on the same basis. The lower advertised nightly rate can produce the higher trip cost.

What to record and verify

Use the same date range, guest count and currency. Record the room subtotal, mandatory fees and displayed total separately. Compare your internal room revenue with your own payout records.
