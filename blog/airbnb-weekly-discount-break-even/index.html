How to compare a weekly Airbnb discount with shorter stays

Compare realistic room revenue and turnover costs rather than nightly rates alone.

Worked example

Seven nights at $150 less a 10% discount produce $945 in room revenue. Compare that with two three-night stays at $150, which produce $900 before turnover, with one night unbooked. The seven-night option also uses one turnover instead of two in this illustration.

What to record and verify

Use equivalent dates, include any payout differences and cleaning costs you actually pay, and check the effective guest rate. Replace both occupancy assumptions with booking information for the specific period before adopting a discount.
