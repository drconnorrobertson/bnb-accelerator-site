When should a last-minute Airbnb discount start?

Use booking lead time and a minimum acceptable room rate to test a late discount.

Worked example

A $180 room rate discounted by 20% becomes $144. If the required room revenue is $150, the largest discount at that starting rate is about 16.7%. A 15% test retains $153 before fees and taxes.

What to record and verify

Choose an arrival window based on how late similar stays are usually booked. Compare a representative set of dates before and after the change. Verify which promotions apply and stop the test if the room rate breaches the floor.
