Fort Walton Beach: Buying the Emerald Coast at a Discount

Published June 15, 2023·6 min read

Ashley and Billy bought a four-bedroom in Fort Walton Beach at $630,000 and had close to 80 nights booked within 21 days of going live. Mark and Farrah bought a four-bedroom in Destin at $950,000. Both properties sell the same water.

The same coast, a different price

Fort Walton Beach sits on the Emerald Coast, the stretch of Florida Panhandle with the clearest water and whitest sand on the Gulf. It is immediately west of Destin and it prices materially below it.

The guest experience is not identical. Destin and 30A have more developed dining, retail and resort infrastructure, and their beaches have a stronger brand. But the water is the same water, and for a family booking a Gulf beach week the difference in experience is smaller than the difference in property price.

That gap is the entire investment thesis. A four-bedroom at $630,000 producing rates within reach of a four-bedroom at $950,000 produces a materially better cash-on-cash return, and the deals we have transacted here reflect that: a $635,000 property on Bedford Place with a $212,048 total entry, and a $320,000 property on Audrey Circle with a $230,692 total entry.

The Eglin demand floor

Eglin Air Force Base is one of the largest military installations in the country by land area, and Hurlburt Field is adjacent. That presence produces non-vacation demand that pure resort markets do not have.

Military relocation, temporary duty assignments, contractor travel, training rotations and family visits generate bookings across the shoulder and off seasons. It is not glamorous demand and it does not command peak summer rates, but it fills weeks that would otherwise be empty.

For underwriting purposes that means a flatter annual curve than Destin's. The peak is lower and the trough is higher, which is generally a better shape for debt service coverage even when the annual total is similar.

Speed to launch matters more here

Ashley and Billy's close to 80 nights in 21 days was not luck. Furnishing, photography, listing copy, pricing setup and channel distribution were sequenced to run in parallel during escrow rather than starting after keys changed hands.

That approach matters everywhere and it matters more in a market with a long season, because there are more weeks available to lose. A property closing in April and going live in July has surrendered spring break, the early summer ramp, and the review accumulation that would have carried it through peak.

Airbnb's search ranking weighs recent booking velocity and review count heavily. A listing that stacks 80 nights and the reviews that follow in its first three weeks enters its first full season ranked like an established property, and that advantage compounds for the entire first year.

What to underwrite carefully

  • Coastal wind and flood insurance, quoted for the specific address. Gulf insurance has repriced sharply and it is a material line, not a footnote.
  • Association assessments where applicable, which can arrive as large one-time bills after a storm season.
  • Hurricane disruption, modeled as three lost peak weeks rather than assumed away.
  • Turnover cost, which runs high in a beach market with weekly changeovers and sand and salt wear.
  • Distance to a public beach access point, which is the largest single rate driver and cannot be changed later.

The four-bedroom threshold

The step from three bedrooms to four is the highest-leverage move in most beach markets, and Fort Walton Beach is no exception. Three bedrooms is a family. Four is two families splitting a week, and the second household roughly doubles willingness to pay because the cost divides.

Above four, five-bedroom inventory is thinner in this submarket than in Destin, which means a five-bedroom faces a smaller competitive set in peak weeks. That thinness supports pricing but also narrows the buyer pool at exit.

For a first purchase, four bedrooms in Fort Walton Beach at a sensible basis is one of the more straightforward entries into Gulf Coast ownership available.

The exit consideration

Value markets have a specific characteristic at sale worth understanding before purchase: the buyer pool is different from the premium market's.

A Destin or 30A property sells to buyers who want that specific location and are comparing it against other premium coastal assets. A Fort Walton Beach property sells to buyers running the same value calculation the original purchaser ran, comparing cash-on-cash returns across submarkets.

That makes the operating record more important at exit. In a premium market, location carries the sale. In a value market, the numbers do, because the buyer is there for the numbers. A property with three years of strong statements, a high review score and documented occupancy sells well. The same house with mediocre performance sells at a discount, because the value thesis it was bought on has not been demonstrated.

The practical implication is to keep clean records from day one. Monthly statements, occupancy and rate history, expense detail and review history are the sale package in a value market, and reconstructing them at listing time is both harder and less credible.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Fort Walton Beach cheaper than Destin?

It sits on the same Emerald Coast stretch with less developed resort infrastructure and a weaker destination brand, but the same water. That price gap without a proportional rate gap is what produces better cash-on-cash returns.

What does Eglin Air Force Base do for the rental market?

It supplies non-vacation demand from relocations, temporary duty assignments, contractor travel and family visits, which fills shoulder and off-season weeks. The result is a flatter annual revenue curve than a pure vacation market.

How quickly can a new beach rental start booking?

Ashley and Billy had close to 80 nights booked within 21 days of going live, achieved by sequencing furnishing, photography and pricing during escrow rather than starting after closing.
