How to review a suggested base rate for a new Airbnb listing

Compare similar stays before assigning a starting rate and separate high-demand dates from an ordinary week.

Worked example

A new two-bedroom listing is compared with a studio and a much larger cabin. Replace those comparisons with properties matching guest capacity and location, and record any major difference in amenities or fees.

What to record and verify

Compare the guest quote for the same dates and checks per listing. Document the assumptions behind the initial rate, then revisit it after you have your own booking and lead-time information.
