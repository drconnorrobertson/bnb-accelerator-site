Airbnb Listing Optimization

Published October 19, 2023·7 min read

A listing has one job: convert an impression into a booking. Most underperforming listings are not badly written, they are badly ordered, and they answer the wrong questions in the wrong sequence.

The two step funnel

Every booking passes through two gates. First, the property has to appear in results and earn a click, which is driven almost entirely by the cover photo, the price, the review score, and the amenity filters you satisfy. Second, the listing page has to convert, which is driven by the photo set, the first two lines of the description, and how quickly a guest can confirm the property fits their group.

Everything else you have built, the furnishing, the amenities, the hospitality, is invisible until step one succeeds. That is why the cover photo does more work than the entire description. See photography that converts.

Title and cover photo

  • Lead with the differentiator, not the property type. Hot Tub, Mountain View, Sleeps 14 does more work than Beautiful Mountain Cabin.
  • Include the number that qualifies the guest. Sleeping capacity or bedroom count filters out mismatched bookings before they become bad reviews.
  • Cover photo shows the single strongest feature, which is frequently not the exterior. If the view is the product, lead with the view.
  • Avoid adjectives that every listing uses. Cozy, stunning, and charming are noise.

The photo set

  1. Cover image: strongest single feature.
  2. Main living space, wide, shot in daylight.
  3. The amenity that drives bookings in your market: hot tub, pool, game room.
  4. Kitchen and dining, showing capacity for a group.
  5. Every bedroom in order, with bed sizes clear.
  6. Bathrooms.
  7. Outdoor space, ideally at the best time of day.
  8. A neighborhood or view shot that establishes location.
  9. Anything unusual that justifies the rate.

Caption every photo. Captions are the cheapest available way to answer questions that would otherwise become inquiries, and inquiries are friction.

Description structure

The first two lines are the only ones most guests read before deciding to expand. Use them to state who the property is for and what makes it different, not to say welcome to our home.

Then, in order: sleeping arrangements stated explicitly bed by bed, the amenities that matter in your market, the location with honest drive times, and finally the practical details such as parking, stairs, road access, and pet policy.

Be conservative about anything a guest can verify. A twelve minute walk described as ten produces a small disappointment. A fifteen minute walk described as five produces a review. See how expectations drive review scores.

The settings that quietly cost bookings

  • Minimum stay set too long in shoulder season, which eliminates the two night bookings that fill a soft calendar.
  • Cleaning fee too high relative to nightly rate, which suppresses conversion on short stays because guests see the total.
  • Instant book disabled, which reduces visibility on most platforms. If screening is the concern, use requirements rather than turning it off entirely. See guest screening.
  • Slow response time, which affects both ranking and conversion.
  • Restrictive cancellation policy on a new listing with no reviews, where you have the least leverage to be strict.

One tax note on minimum stays

Minimum stay settings are also a tax input. Longer minimums raise your average period of customer use, and the short-term rental tax position depends on that average being seven days or less. A seven night minimum in peak season is a defensible revenue decision and a potentially expensive tax decision. Track the running average monthly. See the seven day rule explained.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get my Airbnb listing to rank higher?

Ranking follows performance: click through from search, conversion on the listing page, response speed, review score, and booking velocity. The highest leverage improvements are the cover photo, satisfying the amenity filters guests actually use, and a deliberate below market launch that generates early reviews quickly.

What should an Airbnb title say?

Lead with the differentiator rather than the property type, and include the number that qualifies the guest. Hot Tub, Mountain View, Sleeps 14 does more work than Beautiful Mountain Cabin, because it both attracts the right guest and filters out mismatched bookings before they become bad reviews.

How many photos should an Airbnb listing have?

Around twenty five to thirty five well ordered images, starting with the strongest single feature, then main living space, the market defining amenity, kitchen and dining, every bedroom with bed sizes visible, bathrooms, outdoor space, and a location shot. Caption every photo to preempt inquiries.

Does minimum stay affect short-term rental taxes?

Yes. Minimum stay settings drive your average period of customer use, and the short-term rental tax position depends on that average being seven days or less. A seven night minimum in peak season can be a sound revenue decision and an expensive tax decision at the same time.
