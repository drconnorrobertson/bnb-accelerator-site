Short-Term Rental Bookkeeping and Records

Published July 4, 2026·7 min read

Bookkeeping is the least interesting part of this business and one of the highest leverage. Clean records defend the tax position, accelerate the next financing, and materially raise what an investor buyer will pay at exit. Sloppy records cost money in all three places at once.

Separate everything, from day one

One bank account and one credit card per property. Not per portfolio, per property. Commingling is the single most common bookkeeping failure and it produces two specific problems: you cannot tell which property is actually performing, and reconstructing expense allocations later is expensive and imprecise.

Every dollar in and out of the property runs through those two accounts. Furnishing purchases, supplies, repairs, platform payouts, mortgage payments, insurance, taxes, and travel to the property.

What to track beyond the obvious

  • Revenue gross, not net of platform fees. Platforms report differently and payouts arrive net. Recording payouts as revenue understates both revenue and expenses, which distorts every ratio you calculate.
  • Occupancy tax collected and remitted, separately from income. Platforms collect some of it in some jurisdictions and not others.
  • Capital improvements separately from repairs. These are treated differently and the distinction matters. Keep invoices, not just amounts.
  • Furnishing purchases itemized. These become part of the depreciation analysis. A single lump entry labeled furniture is far less useful to your CPA than an itemized schedule.
  • Mileage and travel to the property, with dates and business purpose recorded contemporaneously.
  • Your participation hours, which are a record with tax consequences rather than a nice to have. See material participation and hour logs.

The document to keep forever

The cost segregation study. It is the record your CPA needs at exit to calculate recapture, and it is the document most likely to be lost in an email thread three years later. Store it with the closing file, not in a downloads folder. See depreciation recapture explained.

Clean records are worth more than most people think

They support the tax position, they speed the next loan, and they raise the price a future investor buyer will pay.

A monthly cadence that takes twenty minutes

  1. Reconcile the property account against the platform payout report.
  2. Categorize the month's transactions, which is nearly automatic with a dedicated card.
  3. Record occupancy, average daily rate, and revenue per available night for the month.
  4. Update the running average length of stay, which is the number that protects the tax position.
  5. Note anything unusual while you still remember it.

That last item matters more than it sounds. A repair invoice from March is meaningless in February of the following year without a note explaining what it was.

The three payoffs

Tax. The short-term rental position rests on documentation: average stay, participation hours, and a defensible expense record. Reconstructed records are exactly what examiners are trained to identify.

Financing. Lenders underwriting the next acquisition want to see clean operating statements. A borrower who produces a tidy trailing twelve month statement in a day gets a faster and often better outcome than one who spends three weeks assembling it.

Exit. An investor buyer is purchasing an income stream. Organized platform dashboards, an expense ledger, and monthly occupancy and rate data sell faster and closer to asking than the identical property with a spreadsheet assembled last week. See when to sell a short-term rental.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate bank account for each short-term rental?

Yes, one account and one card per property rather than per portfolio. Commingling makes it impossible to know which property is actually performing and makes reconstructing expense allocations later both expensive and imprecise.

Should I record Airbnb revenue gross or net of fees?

Gross, with platform fees recorded as an expense. Payouts arrive net, and recording the payout as revenue understates both revenue and expenses, which distorts every operating ratio you calculate and misleads lenders and buyers reading your statements.

What records matter most for the short-term rental tax strategy?

Average length of stay across the year, contemporaneous participation hour logs with specific descriptions and supporting evidence, itemized furnishing purchases, capital improvements separated from repairs, and the cost segregation study itself, which your CPA will need at exit to calculate recapture.

How much time does short-term rental bookkeeping take?

About twenty minutes a month with a dedicated account and card: reconcile against the platform payout report, categorize transactions, record occupancy, average daily rate, and revenue per available night, update the running average length of stay, and note anything unusual while you still remember it.
