How to verify an event pricing override on Airbnb

Test a manual event rate and its stay rules on the actual event dates.

Worked example

A manager sets $260 for Friday and Saturday during an event. A guest arriving Thursday still sees the normal Thursday rate. Test Thursday-to-Sunday and Friday-to-Sunday separately to confirm that each nightly rate and stay restriction is right.

What to record and verify

Keep a record of the event dates and intended rates before publishing. Check the guest quote and calendar after publishing, then check the first night after the event to ensure the premium ends as intended.
