Airbnb Pricing Tool Test Worksheet

Compare every pricing tool against the same property, booking channel, and dates. Record the rate guests see and whether they can actually book the stay before choosing a tool.

Download the pricing test worksheet (CSV)

Choose three dates

  1. Pick a typical midweek night to test the normal rate and discounts.
  2. Pick an event weekend to test premiums, minimum stays, and manual overrides.
  3. Pick a one-night gap between reservations and check whether a guest can book it.

Test rates and restrictions together

For each date, record the suggested nightly rate, the published rate after applicable discounts, and the total quote shown to a guest for the same stay. Note the minimum stay, booking channel, and any rule that overrides your settings. Preview the guest-facing price before enabling automatic sync.

Worked example

Suppose a tool suggests $160 for one open night, and the channel applies a hypothetical 15% discount. The room price becomes $136 before fees and taxes. If a two-night minimum still applies to that one-night gap, the stay may be unbookable at either price. Confirm the final checkout quote and the applicable stay restriction before accepting the recommendation.

Set a launch rule

Choose who can change the price floor, what date range the tool can publish to, and who restores the previous settings if a rate or stay restriction is wrong. Save the baseline before connecting the tool and repeat the check after the first sync.
