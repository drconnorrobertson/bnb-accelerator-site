Is a Hot Tub Worth It for an Airbnb?

Published May 21, 2026·6 min read

In cabin and mountain markets, a hot tub is not an amenity decision. It is a visibility decision, because a large share of guests filter for it before they ever see your listing. The honest analysis includes what it costs to keep one running.

Why it functions as a filter

Guests searching cabin markets routinely apply a hot tub filter. If your property does not have one, you are not in those results, and the bookings you lost never appear in your analytics. That makes the return calculation different from a normal amenity: the question is not how much extra you earn, it is how much of the market you can see.

In corridors like Broken Bow and the Smokies, the effect is strong enough that we treat a hot tub as close to mandatory for cabin inventory. See the Broken Bow guide and the Smokies guide.

Where it matters and where it does not

  • Strong effect: mountain and cabin markets, cold weather markets, and ski adjacent properties, where the tub is part of the experience being purchased.
  • Moderate effect: lake markets and some rural properties, where it supports rate without being a filter.
  • Weak or negative effect: hot climate markets where a pool is the expected amenity, and urban properties where the space could serve another purpose.

The costs nobody quotes

  1. Purchase and installation, including electrical work, which is frequently the larger surprise.
  2. Servicing between guests, which is a per turnover cost and often a separate vendor from your cleaner.
  3. Chemicals and testing, which some jurisdictions regulate for rental properties.
  4. Water and electricity, which are meaningful on a tub heated year round.
  5. Repairs and eventual replacement, since heavy guest use shortens equipment life substantially compared with residential use.
  6. Insurance and liability, because water features raise exposure and belong in your coverage conversation. See the insurance guide.

Operating it without generating reviews

  • Service between every stay, not weekly. A cloudy tub on arrival is a guaranteed review mention.
  • Photograph the tub at completion as part of the turnover verification.
  • Post rules clearly, including capacity, no glass, and shower before use, which reduce both damage and chemistry problems.
  • State the heating time in the house manual. Guests who arrive expecting an instantly hot tub after the cover has been off are the ones who complain.
  • Have a repair path. A broken tub in peak season needs a same week fix, which means a relationship established in advance.

The depreciation angle

A hot tub is typically short life property rather than part of the building shell, and the associated decking, electrical, and site work may also fall into shorter recovery categories in a cost segregation study.

That does not make the amenity free, and it does change the after tax cost of installing one. It is worth discussing the treatment with your CPA alongside the rest of your furnishing and improvement schedule rather than treating it as a pure operating decision. Frequently asked questions

Do hot tubs increase Airbnb bookings?

In cabin, mountain, and cold weather markets they function as a search filter rather than an upgrade, which means properties without one are absent from a large share of searches and never see the bookings they lost. In hot climate markets where a pool is the expected amenity, the effect is much weaker.

What does it cost to run a hot tub at a short-term rental?

Purchase and installation including electrical work, servicing between every stay which is a per turnover cost, chemicals and testing, water and electricity for year round heating, repairs and earlier replacement due to heavy guest use, and higher liability exposure that belongs in your insurance conversation.

How often should a rental hot tub be serviced?

Between every stay rather than weekly. A cloudy or improperly balanced tub on arrival is one of the most reliable ways to generate a review mention, and completion photos of the tub should be part of your turnover verification.

Is a hot tub depreciable on a short-term rental?
