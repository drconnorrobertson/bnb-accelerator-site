What if the seller's Airbnb listing is still live when you buy the STR?

Published September 25, 2026·6 min read

Before buying an operating short-term rental, require a documented plan for the seller's live Airbnb listing. A deed transfer does not give you control of the seller's Airbnb account, listing, messages or reservations. Ask the seller to stop accepting new stays that would occur after closing, produce a current reservation schedule, and resolve each affected guest booking through the platform and the purchase agreement. Confirm the listing's status near closing. If the seller cannot show how post-closing bookings will be handled, protect the closing date or decline an offer priced as a seamless operating handoff. Book a call to plan the STR listing handoff before you make an offer.

Separate stopping new bookings from resolving confirmed stays

Airbnb says a host may unlist a home indefinitely to remove it from search and stop new reservations, yet confirmed reservations remain the host's responsibility. Its guidance also warns that scheduling an unlisting for future dates does not itself block the calendar now, and that a listing with upcoming reservations cannot simply be permanently removed. Therefore, a seller's promise to 'delete the listing at closing' is not a complete handoff plan. Ask for the exact action, date, responsible person and proof of status.

Airbnb's account-transfer guidance says an account cannot be transferred to a different host and reservations cannot be transferred to a new host or account, including through a trip-change request. Do not assume that buying the house includes the listing, reviews or booking obligations. The future-reservations guide addresses the guest and financial ledger; this page handles the distinct risk that the seller-controlled public listing keeps selling dates after title passes.

Request a listing-shutdown evidence packet

Before removing contingencies, ask the seller for the public listing URL and ID, screenshots or an authorized export of the calendar and all confirmed stays spanning closing, the date new post-closing availability was blocked, and proof the listing was unlisted or otherwise no longer accepting future stays. Recheck from a public browser and, where possible, have the seller demonstrate status in their account; a public search result alone may lag or vary. Keep the seller's account credentials private and do not ask to take them over.

Create a row for every stay touching or following closing: reservation ID held by the seller, guest arrival and departure, payments and deposits, cancellation or accommodation plan, who will communicate with the guest, and a deadline for written resolution. Coordinate with the broker, closing agent, local counsel, platform and any manager so the property is not promised to two parties. Ask the seller to disclose new reservations or date changes until closing. A one-time spreadsheet is stale as soon as the calendar changes.

Put the cutoff and verification into the purchase timeline

Ask local counsel to draft transaction-specific seller cooperation and disclosure duties, a no-new-post-closing-bookings cutoff, a schedule of existing reservations, guest communications, allocation of payouts and refunds, and remedies if the schedule changes. Do not improvise a clause from an online template: vacation-rental statutes, purchase contracts and platform policies can differ by jurisdiction. If a confirmed stay crosses closing, counsel should determine whether it can be honored lawfully and on whose account and insurance, or whether the seller must cancel it through the platform. Airbnb tells hosts to cancel promptly if they cannot honor a stay and warns against asking guests to cancel for them; host cancellation consequences may apply.

Illustrative timeline only: if closing is October 15 and the seller's account shows a guest arriving October 18, a screenshot saying the listing was unlisted on October 14 does not resolve that booking. The buyer needs a documented guest plan before closing, plus a final calendar refresh and listing-status check on closing day. The buyer should not assume a new listing can inherit that reservation. If the seller is unwilling to resolve it, seek a protected extension or reject the handoff rather than accepting an undocumented guest arrival.

Underwrite the buyer's launch, not the seller's account

Model the seller's verified trailing revenue as historical evidence, not as a purchased Airbnb account or guaranteed future payouts. Build a buyer-controlled launch plan with lawful STR permission, a new listing, photos, management setup and a realistic review ramp. The review-continuity guide covers reputation, while the prior-removal guide covers a different platform-enforcement problem. An old seller listing still being bookable is a transaction-handoff problem even if neither party has been suspended.

Proceed when the seller has stopped new post-closing bookings, each existing stay has a lawful and documented resolution, and the buyer's own launch economics work. Renegotiate if a gap in bookings or added guest-resolution cost reduces supported value. Delay only under valid contract protection for a specific missing confirmation. Reject if seller cooperation is absent, guests could arrive without authority to host them, or the purchase depends on account and reservation transfer Airbnb does not permit. Frequently asked questions

Does buying the property give me the seller's Airbnb listing?

No. Airbnb says accounts and reservations cannot transfer to a new host or account. Plan a buyer-controlled listing and verify the seller's old listing stops taking new post-closing stays.

Is unlisting enough if guests are already booked?

No. Airbnb says unlisting stops new reservations but confirmed stays remain. Resolve every stay spanning or following closing separately.

Can the seller delete a listing with upcoming reservations?

Airbnb says a home listing cannot be permanently removed until upcoming reservations are complete. The seller can unlist it while bookings are addressed, subject to the platform's rules.

What if the seller will not stop accepting post-closing bookings?

Ask counsel and the broker to use valid contract protection and a defined cutoff. Delay or reject an unsupported handoff; do not assume the buyer can control the seller's account after closing.
