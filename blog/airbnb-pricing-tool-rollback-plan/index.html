How to prepare a rollback before changing Airbnb pricing tools

Preserve the current calendar rules so you can restore an incorrect release.

Worked example

Before connecting a new tool, export or record the next 60 days of rates and minimum stays, including event overrides. If the first sync changes unapproved dates, use the saved record to identify exactly what needs restoration.

What to record and verify

Assign a person to stop the sync and a person to confirm the restored guest quote. Test an event date and an ordinary date after rollback. Record the last successful settings and the reason the test stopped.
