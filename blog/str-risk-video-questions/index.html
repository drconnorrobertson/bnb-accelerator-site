How can an STR buyer turn a warning video into a downside plan?

Name the event, estimate the cash effect and decide what evidence could change the purchase decision. Model a slow launch, weaker demand, a major repair and a restriction on rental use. Keep reserves available independently of projected tax savings.

What belongs in the purchase file?

Keep the property address, source dates, seller records, financing quotes, vendor scopes, legal-use findings and unresolved questions in one file. Identify who owns each follow-up and the date it must be resolved. Separate a verified fact from a forecast or an example described in a recording.

How should you compare two proposed deals?

Use the same period, cash-investment definition and operating assumptions. Include acquisition charges, repair and furnishing costs, opening reserves and ongoing service fees. A higher revenue headline can still leave less cash after expenses and debt service.
