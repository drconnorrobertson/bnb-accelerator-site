How to Finance an Airbnb Investment Property

Published August 10, 2026·Updated October 8, 2026·9 min read

To finance an Airbnb investment property, first find a loan that permits your actual property and rental plan, then compare the complete cash requirement and debt obligations. A lower advertised rate or down payment does not make an ineligible product available. High W-2 income, business income or substantial capital can help support a purchase, but none replaces property eligibility or an accepted borrower file.

This guide compares six financing paths for buyers preparing to purchase an STR. Use it to request address-specific written terms, not as a current rate sheet or a promise of approval. Separate the purchase mortgage from borrowed down-payment funds and from a refinance you hope to obtain after closing.

Conventional investment property loan

Ask the lender which agency or portfolio program applies, what borrower income it accepts, how it treats existing debts and which property conditions remain. Obtain the actual down payment, closing cash and reserves for your file. A generic percentage or number of reserve months is not a quote.

Fannie Mae's specific STR-income guidance, dated September 2, 2026, permits documented qualifying rent for eligible one-unit investment properties with lawful STR use. Purchase evidence can include a long-term Form 1007 or specified validated comparable STR data. The lender must document a current housing payment; a positive calculated result offsets PITIA rather than adding unlimited qualifying income. Ask about implementation and overlays. This is not permission to use any seller projection or a rule for every conventional lender.

The conventional STR-income acceptance worksheet develops those evidence and calculation gates. Here the purchase decision is whether the lender accepts your file and the resulting cash budget is affordable, not whether conventional financing is always better for the first two properties.

Second-home financing

Use this path only when your genuine occupancy and management plan fit the program. Fannie Mae's occupancy guidance requires borrower use for part of the year, a one-unit property suitable for year-round occupancy, exclusive control and no agreement giving a management firm occupancy control. Its rental-income clarification also requires that rent not qualify the borrower and that all remaining second-home requirements be satisfied.

Disclose intended rental activity, personal use and the actual manager agreement. Do not misstate use to obtain another financing label. A lender classification does not establish tax treatment, lawful STR permission or insurance coverage. The second-home rental-intent guide addresses the narrower approval packet. Compare pricing only after the lender has assessed the real use.

DSCR loans

For a proposed debt-service-coverage-ratio loan, ask for the lender's written definition of qualifying income and debt service. Which rental evidence is accepted, which expenses are included, and what happens if the property's accepted rent is below the seller forecast? Do not substitute your operating cash-flow model for its qualification calculation.

Obtain the actual documentation, credit, liquidity, ownership, property and portfolio requirements. Do not assume every DSCR product removes tax returns, all borrower review or every limit on financed properties. Compare payment, points, required reserves, recourse and any prepayment charge using that proposal. No universal current rate premium or minimum ratio is asserted here. Use the DSCR guide to organize the lender questions, then verify terms with the lender.

Portfolio and local bank lending

For a bank-held proposal, request the permitted geography and property use, borrower and entity requirements, accepted rental evidence, amortization, maturity date and renewal conditions. Relationship lending is not a promise of approval or a reason to assume a bank accepts one market but rejects another.

If the quote has a balloon or short maturity, model the amount due and a no-refinance scenario before choosing it. A longer amortization does not necessarily mean the loan lasts that long. Record whether an extension is contractual, discretionary or absent; do not count a future renewal as committed financing.

HELOC on your primary residence

A HELOC is a separate source of borrowed capital, not the STR purchase mortgage. CFPB's HELOC guidance explains that the home secures the borrowing, rates are usually variable, further draws can be frozen in specified circumstances and payments can increase at repayment. Failure to repay can put the home at risk.

Ask both lenders whether the proposed source and related payment are acceptable. Record actual drawable funds, draw conditions, payment changes and the repayment deadline. Test household obligations and both loans against a separately funded low-revenue interval. An intended refinance or future tax benefit is not a repayment commitment. Do not count an undrawn line as cleared cash or assume eighteen months is a universally safe bridge period.

Delayed financing

A cash purchase followed by a refinance is two transactions. Fannie Mae's delayed-financing exception has original-purchase, borrower, title, source-of-funds and cash-out requirements. The loan is limited by documented investment and applicable ratios based on current appraisal, with additional restrictions when purchase funds were borrowed or gifted. Meeting an exception is not a promise to recover the full purchase price.

Budget purchase and launch using funds actually available now. Obtain a prospective refinance assessment and calculate net proceeds after its costs and required paydowns. The delayed-financing net-proceeds worksheet develops smaller or unavailable refinance outcomes. Do not assume a cash offer wins or fund another obligation with proceeds not yet approved and received.

Hypothetical example, not an offer or required minimum: a buyer allocates $300,000 of cleared capital to a $700,000 purchase. Available quote A provides a $525,000 loan, requiring $175,000 price cash; closing fees and prepaids total $18,000. A $20,000 already-paid deposit is credited toward the $193,000 settlement allocation, so remaining settlement cash is $173,000, not $193,000 plus the deposit. Add $32,000 setup, $10,000 opening carrying costs and $45,000 retained reserves: total allocation $280,000, leaving $20,000 unallocated.

Available quote B supplies a $490,000 loan with $14,000 closing fees and prepaids. Its settlement allocation is $224,000, including the same credited deposit; remaining settlement cash is $204,000. With the same $87,000 setup, carrying and reserve allocation, total required capital is $311,000, an $11,000 gap. Even an assumed $400 lower monthly payment saves only $4,800 over twelve months; it does not supply missing settlement funds. Verify actual payment and whole-term cost independently.

If A also requires $12,000 of distinct repairs omitted from setup, its allocation becomes $292,000 and margin $8,000. A further non-overlapping $9,000 delay cost creates a $1,000 shortfall. The $45,000 reserve remains protected in every branch. These invented scenarios illustrate cash timing, not lender eligibility, investment return, client experience or tax results.

Set the financing envelope before the offer

Proceed only with an honestly disclosed, permitted financing path whose conditions, dates and funded downside you understand. Renegotiate price or select another genuinely available proposal when the budget fails. Delay under valid protection while decisive evidence remains outstanding; reject a financing thesis that depends on inaccurate occupancy or uncommitted future cash.

Resolve intended ownership with the lender, qualified counsel and CPA before applying. Ask about the actual documents before any later transfer; this page does not determine a transfer's legal or tax consequences. Use the acquisition sequence and the high-income buying hub to connect funding with the rest of the purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Can you get a conventional mortgage for an Airbnb?

Potentially. Fannie Mae permits documented qualifying rent for eligible one-unit investment STR purchases under its specific requirements. Ask the lender about implementation, accepted evidence, current housing payment and all remaining borrower and property conditions; a seller forecast alone is not approval.

What is the lowest down payment for a short-term rental?

There is no universal minimum for every STR purchase. Obtain an address-specific quote for a product that permits your actual use. Compare closing and launch cash plus retained reserves, and exclude a cheaper second-home quote if the disclosed occupancy and management plan is ineligible.

How many mortgages can you have on investment properties?

Ask the lender how its current program counts your financed properties and related debts and whether additional reserves or restrictions apply. Do not assume a DSCR or bank-held loan eliminates every portfolio limit or has no effect on a later lender's borrower review.

Questions to resolve before committing to this purchase

How do I compare STR mortgage lender fees?

Compare dated quotes using the same loan amount, occupancy representation, lock period and points. A lower rate can require more cash than the buyer can safely allocate.

How do I document STR purchase funds?

List the source of each dollar for deposit, down payment and fees. Ask the lender what tracing it needs before moving funds between accounts.

How do I choose STR loan occupancy classification?

Describe actual personal use and rental plans to the lender. Ask which product permits that use; do not select a classification solely for a better advertised rate.

How do I budget STR rate lock extensions?

Match the lock expiration to appraisal, title and underwriting deadlines. Obtain extension pricing and assign responsibility for delays in the financing comparison.

How do I compare STR loan cash requirements?

Build a common cash schedule for competing quotes, including points, prepaids and retained reserves. Compare what remains available after settlement, not just the down payment.

Record the evidence, responsible reviewer, cash effect and transaction deadline for any unresolved item. Bring those findings to the STR acquisition call before deciding whether to proceed, revise the offer or exercise available contract protection.
