Why an Airbnb one-night gap may be unbookable

Check minimum stays and arrival restrictions before discounting an isolated night.

Worked example

Tuesday is free between two reservations. A two-night minimum blocks the one-night stay even when the nightly rate is lower. A Tuesday check-in restriction would still block it after a minimum-stay change.

What to record and verify

Search for the exact interval from the guest side. Check available nights, minimum stay by arrival day, and both check-in and checkout rules in that order. Apply any exception only to the intended gap and retest neighboring dates.
