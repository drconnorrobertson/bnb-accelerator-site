Do Airbnb Reviews Transfer When You Sell the Property?

Published September 23, 2026·Updated October 7, 2026·6 min read

Do not buy an STR as if the seller's Airbnb reviews automatically convey with the deed. A favorable review history can be evidence of past guest experience without being an asset the buyer can use. Before paying a goodwill premium, separate what physically transfers, what requires permission, and what the buyer's own operating case supports without inherited reviews. Substantial available capital does not make uncertain account continuity a sound acquisition assumption.

Bring the seller's claimed review premium and your independent launch case to an acquisition call. BNB Accelerator assists within its contracted acquisition scope; Airbnb and independent transaction advisers determine their respective rules and approvals.

Account ownership is not a furnishing or deed transfer

Airbnb's current account-transfer guidance says account ownership cannot transfer to a different host and reservations cannot transfer to a new host or account. It distinguishes co-host designation from transfer and does not allow a trip-change request to hand reservation management to a new owner. Buying login credentials, retaining a manager or changing possession does not establish an exception.

Do not interpret a broker's promise that reviews will stay as platform approval. Identify the actual account owner, listing and proposed arrangement; ask Airbnb about the transaction before relying on continuity. Counsel should evaluate the sale documents, lawful operating arrangement and rights to use other assets. A platform response is not a permit, insurance acceptance or a management agreement for the buyer.

The seller's review history is not automatically buyer-owned goodwill merely because guests reviewed the same address. This guide does not promise review migration, a new-listing ranking, a retention percentage or a procedure to transfer an account. Model a buyer launch without inherited reviews unless actual permitted facts support the specific alternative; do not assume every unusual transaction has the same outcome.

Record the named reviewer, unresolved question and decision deadline beside each row. A favorable answer for one asset does not approve all the others. Permission to use seller-owned photographs would not establish ownership of account reviews. Likewise, a manager agreeing to serve the buyer would not prove that the seller's platform history becomes the buyer's history.

Read review themes alongside physical and operating diligence. Compliments about a view may describe an enduring property feature; comments about a specific host's responsiveness describe a service the buyer must separately arrange. Neither proves a measured causal effect on revenue. Do not convert a rating difference into a percentage revenue uplift without appropriate evidence, or accuse a seller of misconduct because continuity is unsupported.

Compare the same property without assuming inherited reviews

Build the buyer's own conservative opening case using permitted use, condition, supported comparable demand, actual fees, readiness and costs. Do not copy mature seller performance into the first buyer year solely because the historical rating is attractive. The seller forecast bridge tests each claimed improvement; the manager-retention comparison isolates recurring service economics.

Keep review goodwill separate from the future-booking premium worksheet. The calendar owner already compares permitted buyer receipts with a fresh-launch alternative. Do not count the same assumed booking advantage once as calendar value, again as review goodwill and again in an income-based price increase.

For an illustrative cash test, assume $300,000 accessible acquisition funds. A complete hypothetical purchase-and-launch allocation is $270,000, including $40,000 protected operational reserve and all originally modeled closing and setup items. It excludes a proposed $24,000 additional purchase outlay attributed by the seller to goodwill. The allocation is stipulated as cash for this exercise; a real financed price change needs the lender's actual cash calculation.

Make the decision before relying on continuity

Proceed when the real estate, lawful operating path and independently supported buyer launch work at the price, without an unsupported review assumption. Reprice a viable purchase when replacement branding, operator terms or additional opening costs change the economics. Seek a delay only under valid contractual protection while a decisive platform or adviser answer remains outstanding. Reject a purchase that requires prohibited account transfer or unverified review-driven earnings to meet the buyer's requirements.

Keep authorized source records and platform responses in the private deal file. Use listing identifiers and redacted relevant evidence rather than guest names, passwords or public copies of seller documents. The seller-file acceptance index helps retain current versions; the reservation-closing ledger handles guest obligations independently.

Compare the acquisition shortlist with the no-inherited-reviews case and funded opening plan. For high-income buyers, keep tax suitability separate from an unsupported goodwill claim. Frequently asked questions

Do Airbnb reviews automatically convey with a property sale?

Do not assume they do. Airbnb prohibits transfer of account ownership to a different host, and the deed does not establish review continuity. Ask the platform about the actual arrangement and model an independent buyer launch.

Does keeping the seller’s manager prove I can use the reviews?

No. Manager service acceptance and platform account rules are separate questions. Confirm actual buyer terms, account ownership and the permitted operating plan before assigning value.

Should I pay a separate premium for the seller’s review history?

Only a supported purchase case can justify the actual price. Treat reviews as historical evidence, test a no-inherited-reviews case and avoid counting the same booking benefit as both calendar and goodwill value.
