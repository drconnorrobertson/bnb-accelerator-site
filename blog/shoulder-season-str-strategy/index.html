The direct answer

Change the offer, calendar, and operating cost to the actual shoulder guest rather than simply discounting the peak-season listing.

Average annual rate and occupancy conceal the calendar decisions that create them. Price, minimum stay, lead time, and availability must be tested at date level.

Evidence to collect before deciding

Guest origin, trip purpose, stay length, weekday demand, weather, amenity availability, utilities, and comparable messaging.

Decision file: preserve the source, the date checked, and who confirmed it. Define the rule, the data window, and the review date before changing the calendar. Measure contribution and displacement, not occupancy or nightly rate in isolation.

Run the decision test

Define a shoulder segment, package only real value, and compare contribution after discounts and seasonal operating costs.

Use the downside version first. If the decision only works when every unresolved item lands favorably, the property has no diligence margin.

Write the one-page decision memo

Then identify the document or event that could reverse that conclusion.
