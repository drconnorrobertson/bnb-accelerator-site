How to review the price when an Airbnb cancellation reopens dates

Check both the reopened dates and the rules still attached to them.

Worked example

A Saturday booking cancels two days before arrival. Advance-notice rules or a two-night minimum can still prevent a guest booking Saturday alone, even though it appears open on the host calendar.

What to record and verify

Confirm the cancellation in the booking record, then check the reopened span from the guest side. Review arrival lead time, adjacent reservations and available cleaning before testing any rate change.
