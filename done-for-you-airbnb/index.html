What done-for-you actually means

Done-for-you is not a marketing phrase. It is a description of scope. When you hire BNB Accelerator, our team handles the market analysis, the deal sourcing, the underwriting, the offer, the negotiation, the inspection coordination, the closing, the design direction, the property manager pairing, and the tax strategy introduction. Your role is to make decisions and provide capital. Everything operational is ours.

This is not coaching. There are no modules, no homework, no weekly calls where you learn theory. We do the work. You own the asset. That distinction matters because it is the difference between buying a course and buying a service.

Who this is built for

High-income earners who want real estate exposure without a second career. Business owners who understand delegation. Professionals working 50 to 70 hour weeks who cannot add property management to their schedule. Out-of-state investors who want to own in high-performing markets hundreds of miles from where they live.

The common thread is capital plus constraint. You have the resources to invest but not the bandwidth to operate, and you are smart enough to know that hiring experts produces better results than learning from scratch.

What our clients own after 12 months

A typical client working with BNB Accelerator closes on their first property within 45 to 90 days of engagement. Within 12 months, most are operating one to three cash-flowing short-term rentals, fully managed by professional operators, generating $3,000 to $12,000 per month in combined net revenue. They have never coordinated a cleaning, answered a guest message at 2 AM, or troubleshot a broken water heater.

The properties are real. The cash flow is real. And the time commitment -- roughly 15 minutes a month reviewing a dashboard -- is real too. That is what done-for-you means in practice, not in theory.
