What “best” means in Scottsdale

The strongest candidate in Scottsdale, Arizona is not necessarily the listing with the most projected revenue. Compare the purchase price and total setup budget with evidence of guest demand, legal operating permission, insurance, cash reserves and an exit plan. A listing that wins on one metric can fail the complete purchase test.

Begin with pool homes suited to winter and spring groups. Then compare larger homes with outdoor entertaining space and homes with a lower-cost summer operating plan. These are property profiles to investigate, not a list of homes currently for sale or a promise of returns. The Scottsdale market guide explains the demand pattern and local market context.

Build a comparable set before choosing a property

Select five to ten bookable rentals competing for the same Scottsdale guest. Match bedroom count, legal occupancy, location, parking and amenity tier. Note rates by month, available dates and whether the comparison property has reviews or an established ranking. Do not use a premium home to justify the revenue of an ordinary listing.

For a property already operating, ask for reservation-level records, monthly platform payouts, cancellations, owner-blocked nights and an expense ledger. For a new property, model the launch period separately. Run a downside case with lower occupancy, higher insurance and an opening delay. Use the revenue projection framework to make assumptions visible.

Questions for a Scottsdale real estate agent

A local licensed buyer agent can help locate inventory, arrange showings, write offers and explain the local transaction process. Ask for written evidence of the exact parcel’s short-term-rental eligibility and identify who will confirm it with the authority. Ask the agent to distinguish verified seller revenue from a marketing estimate.

Ask how offer contingencies will protect investigation of pool safety, cooling expense, city registration and HOA restrictions. Discuss title, inspections, financing, association documents and any seller bookings separately. Representation and compensation depend on the agent and brokerage agreement; confirm them before signing. See how an agent and acquisition team divide the work.

Choose a deal, then set a maximum offer

Price the entire project: purchase, closing, repairs, furnishings, permits, insurance, carrying costs and working capital. Check pool safety, cooling expense, city registration and HOA restrictions. Reconcile the seller’s numbers and speak with the relevant local authority and association before relying on permission to operate.

What are the best short-term rental properties to buy in Scottsdale?
