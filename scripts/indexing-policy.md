# Search inventory policy

`curate-indexing.py public` runs after source generation and discovery repairs.
It modifies deployment output only. All 10,802 screening archive routes remain
available; no record is deleted or redirected.

Search-facing property snapshots must have an advanced screening status, market,
recorded price, projected income, bedroom count, and an additional numeric reviewer
field. The October 8 source currently yields 93 property owners. The main library,
advanced-candidate directory, market index and market owners with at least three
selected records remain indexable (13 directory routes currently). Full native
models that pass the separate publication validator are also retained.

Other archive and pagination routes receive `noindex, follow` and are removed
from every deployed sitemap. Their source dates, disclaimers and financial fields
remain intact. This is editorial search selection, not approval of a purchase or
verification of returns. Never fill missing underwriting inputs with invented data.

The overview links directly to every selected property. It preserves access to
the full archive under a disclosure control. The build fails on an empty cohort,
a missing selected sitemap owner or a utility URL leaking into a sitemap.

Index counts may fall as utility records leave search. Evaluate the eligible
cohort's indexing, non-brand visibility and qualified inquiries rather than the
raw total of indexed archive pages. Actual improvement requires recrawling and
measurement; sitemap submission alone does not establish success.
