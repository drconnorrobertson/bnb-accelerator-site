## Narrower research candidate — not approved

A fee-simple STR purchase with an existing third-party communications lease may require distinguishing the purchased property's rent entitlement from rent already sold or assigned, and documenting the actual postclosing payee. This is incoming rent, unlike the buyer's outgoing ground rent or solar payment. A public-source keyword screen found no dedicated cell-tower lease answer, but absence of a keyword match alone does not establish distinct intent. A broader substantive overlap review and source/document verification remain necessary before scoring or drafting.

Potential original decision support: separate the land, leasehold/recorded access rights and rent entitlement in a rights-and-payee register. An illustrative property cash case could compare $18,000 supported annual rent less $3,000 distinct owner obligations ($15,000) with zero transferable rent. It must not add a seller's historical buyout to buyer proceeds, assume lender recognition, or capitalize unsupported rent into an offer. These are proposed worksheet inputs, not market figures, lender terms or a qualified guide.
