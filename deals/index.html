25 closed deals, with the actual numbers

Returns on individual deals range from 1.84% to 24.32%.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average cash-on-cash return on a BNB Accelerator deal?

Across the 25 deals in the published tracker, the average cash-on-cash return is 13.3% and the median is 14.1%. 4 of 25 deals returned 20% or more, 10 returned 15% or more, and 17 returned 10% or more. The full range is 1.84% to 24.32%.

How much does a BNB Accelerator property cost?

The average purchase price across the tracker is $892,596, ranging from $415,000 to $1,600,000. Total entry cost, which includes down payment, closing costs and the design and enhancement budget, averages $234,233.

How much cash flow does a BNB Accelerator property produce?

Average annual cash flow across the tracker is $33,417, and the 25 properties produce $835,413 combined. Cash flow on individual deals ranges from $4,602 to $60,987.

How much do you spend on design and furnishing?

The average design and enhancement budget across the tracker is $107,447. That is a separate line from the down payment and closing costs, and all three together make up the total entry cost.

Are these results typical?

No. These are the actual figures for these specific properties, published because they are documented. They are not typical, not projections, and not a promise of what any other property will do. Results depend on purchase price, financing, market performance, management quality and your own tax situation. Real estate involves risk, including loss of principal.

Does this tracker show every BNB Accelerator deal?

No. It is the subset of closed purchases for which full financials have been documented and cleared for publication. BNB Accelerator has closed over 500 homes for more than 260 clients since 2021; this page covers the deals where every figure can be shown.
