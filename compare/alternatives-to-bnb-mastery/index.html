Alternatives to BNB Mastery

Do you want to learn how to build an STR business yourself, or have an acquisition team execute your first purchase?

Discuss My First STRCheck My Purchase Cash

Start with the outcome you need

BNB Mastery offers courses and coaching, including co-hosting education. It fits buyers who want to learn and apply the operating skill themselves.

For a first STR, decide whether you want to select a particular home, own an interest in managed assets, or learn to execute the purchase yourself. These paths involve different control, capital and work. BNB Accelerator coordinates sourcing, underwriting, purchase and launch for a client-approved property; the signed scope defines the work included.

Three credible paths to compare

Path 1

BNB Accelerator

Fits: People with purchase capital who want a team to source, underwrite, and coordinate the acquisition.

Tradeoff: This is a property purchase with financing, carrying costs, and ownership risk.

Path 2

BNB Mastery

Fits: People who want coaching, community, and a skill they can apply to future deals or co-hosting.

Tradeoff: Education does not purchase, finance, or launch a property on your behalf.

Path 3

DIY with local specialists

Fits: Buyers comfortable assembling a team while learning from free and paid resources.

Tradeoff: Your time and the quality of your own decisions become the main constraints.

Operating skill versus property ownership

Write your desired outcome before selecting a program: buying a deed, leasing with permission to sublet, or managing someone else’s rental. Those require different capital and agreements. A co-hosting course can suit a service-business goal without being an acquisition alternative. If ownership is the goal, retain a lender-approved budget and a buyer-owned purchase schedule.

Build this decision file

  1. Desired ownership or business model
  2. Current curriculum and coaching scope
  3. Property-purchase capital plan

Make the total cash comparable

Use the same property price and current financing terms for direct-purchase proposals. Add down payment, closing, furnishing, repairs, quoted service fees and retained reserves. For an offering, separate the subscription amount from possible future capital obligations and liquidity restrictions. A lower fee is meaningful only when the missing work and buyer responsibilities are visible.

Calculate first-STR purchase cash and enter the fees actually quoted to you. Use conservative revenue and address-specific expense evidence; neither a provider’s case study nor potential tax savings funds your down payment.

Choose before you sign

At the end of the engagement, what work has been done for me versus what have I learned to do myself?

Proceed when the documents settle that question and the capital, scope and operating plan fit. Request a revision if duties or charges are vague. Pause when legal use, financing, insurance or operator capacity can change the purchase. Compare proposals on the same decision file and keep the right to reject an unsuitable property.
