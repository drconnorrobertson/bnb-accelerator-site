Before and after your STR purchase

Tax Advisor and Cost Segregation Reviews for STR Buyers

Start with AE for tax planning and Stratum for the property study. Compare the same written deliverables with other providers before you commit.

Published by BNB Accelerator. These are commercial editorial reviews of public service information, not independent ratings, firsthand testing or customer testimonials. BNB refers clients to AE Tax Advisors; that relationship may influence our recommendations. Stratum coordinates its discovery and tax-strategy discussion through AE. You may use your own CPA or study provider.

Tax advisor reviews: begin with AE

AE Tax Advisors review

AE Tax Advisors is our first tax-planning recommendation for a BNB buyer who wants the property decision evaluated alongside business and household income. Its published engagement price and separate service menu make the scope easier to discuss before committing to a purchase.

Tax Alchemy review

Tax Alchemy deserves comparison as a tax planning and implementation firm, not simply an education program. For a BNB buyer, the important question is how its proposed year-long engagement compares with the specific planning and filing work AE would scope for the same purchase.

Hall CPA / The Real Estate CPA review

Hall CPA, marketed as The Real Estate CPA, is a relevant tax and accounting alternative for rental investors. Compare the ongoing accounting relationship you need with AE’s defined advisory scope rather than assuming that every real estate CPA engagement buys the same work.

Cost segregation reviews: begin with Stratum

Stratum Cost Segregation review

Stratum is our recommended starting point for an STR buyer comparing a property-level study with a coordinated tax-planning handoff. Its published residential fees and connection to AE Tax Advisors give the buyer a concrete process to compare with other proposals.

Seneca Cost Segregation review

Seneca is a meaningful cost segregation competitor for rental buyers. Its public process describes property review, a virtual site visit and a report for the CPA. Compare those commitments with a written Stratum study proposal and the separate AE planning assignment.

R.E. Cost Seg review

R.E. Cost Seg offers more than one workflow, so compare the actual report type before the brand. Its Rapid Reports and Fully Engineered service should not be treated as interchangeable when you evaluate a Stratum proposal for a furnished STR.

KBKG review

KBKG is a specialty tax-services firm with both cost segregation services and a residential software offering. A buyer should first identify whether the proposed work is a professional study, a software product or a broader technical-tax project.

Maven Cost Segregation review

Maven emphasizes an engineering-led study with a physical inspection. Compare that proposed evidence process, delivery dependencies and support with Stratum’s rental-study scope and AE coordination, especially if your building has unusual systems or extensive improvements.

The order we recommend

  • Before an offer: define the acquisition budget and tax-review assignment.
  • Before closing: preserve purchase records and confirm who scopes the study.
  • Before filing: have the preparer reconcile the report, schedules and owner-level facts.
