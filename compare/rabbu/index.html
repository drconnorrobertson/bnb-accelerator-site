Free research versus a paid search engagement

Screen a listing with the marketplace and calculator, then request the scope of any optional search consultant. Mark which assumptions are estimates and which have been checked against records. Require a named owner for permit questions, inspections and closing tasks. Compare the paid tier with another written acquisition proposal rather than equating every tool user with a managed purchase client.

Request these records

  • Exact tier and compensation disclosure
  • Verified versus estimated inputs
  • Search and closing deliverables

Use one property and one budget

Give each acquisition provider the same active listing, available cash, financing assumptions and intended operating model. Require an address-level permission plan, comparable revenue evidence, an expense model and a maximum offer under a downside case. If you are evaluating education or an offering, compare it to that purpose instead of expecting it to perform a transaction.

Budget the complete purchase with the first-STR cash planner. Verify which professionals you contract with and who monitors contingencies. Financing approval, permits, bookings and investment outcomes remain property- and buyer-specific.

Current evidence: identify the party doing the purchase work

Rabbu’s marketplace presents historical income and modeled projections as different listing inputs, alongside agent, lender and concierge connections. Its current concierge destination is an STR Search consultant profile, describing market selection, shortlists, underwriting and launch guidance while retaining separate licensed agent and lender roles. Do not assume the platform and consultant are one contracting entity, or present the connected consultant as an entirely unrelated alternative.

The calculator documentation describes editable comparables and gross revenue that excludes cleaning and specified platform/service/management fees. This is screening support, not confirmed seller receipts, net owner cash or legal-use approval. Neither a listing badge nor a public testimonial independently establishes the investment case for your address.

BNB’s published process describes sourcing, underwriting, purchase coordination and designer/operator pairing. Final decisions and capital remain with the buyer; lender, legal/tax advisers and daily operator have separate roles. Ask for BNB’s actual signed scope too. This comparison has not authenticated an executed client contract for either route and does not establish provider fees, refund rights or guaranteed outcomes.

Original work sample: move a discovered listing into an offer decision

Ask the consultant or acquisition team to complete this evidence-to-offer register for one real candidate before relying on the initial screen. Keep each source, unresolved item, owner, due date and your approval in the file.

  1. Receipts: Label history versus estimate, the dates covered, lodging versus cleaning/tax receipts and owner-use assumptions. Reconcile authorized seller records where supplied; do not publish guest or banking information. Explain excluded comparables rather than treating all nearby homes as interchangeable.
  2. Expenses: Replace placeholder insurance, tax, repair and operating costs with address-specific evidence. Identify excluded work, vendor invoices and platform/manager fees once. Name who updates the model when inspection changes the scope.
  3. Representation: Identify the consultant, broker and lender separately, along with compensation recipients, referral relationships, deliverables and payment triggers. Ask which fees survive a rejected candidate and which work transfers to the next search. Do not presume a free platform introduction includes paid execution.
  4. Permission and deadlines: Name who obtains municipal/HOA documents, escalates unresolved rights and monitors contract dates. Identify who delivers valid notices and who makes the final buyer decision. A sourcing team’s analysis does not itself create a cancellation right.
  5. Offer and launch: Show the cash ceiling and downside operating case separately. Name who submits an approved offer, accepts opening work and obtains a contracted operator. A service's estimate of speed is not a funded launch schedule.

Use the responsibility matrix for assignment and the seller payout guide for history. Missing evidence calls for clarification, not an accusation about either firm. Preserve the buyer’s authority to decline an unsupported purchase.

Worked cash ceiling: a price cut does not free its full amount

Hypothetical worksheet—not Rabbu or BNB pricing, a loan offer or a client result: Assume $270,000 spendable cash, a $600,000 property and a lender-approved structure requiring 25% down. Stipulate $18,000 closing charges, $30,000 furnishing, $20,000 repairs, $40,000 retained reserves and a fictional $10,000 acquisition-service charge. Fixed non-down-payment allocation is $118,000. With $150,000 down, total allocation is $268,000, leaving $2,000.

Inspection now identifies a distinct $18,000 repair increase, making fixed allocation $136,000 and total capital $286,000: $16,000 short. With all those fixed amounts and the 25% down structure unchanged, a $16,000 price reduction frees only $4,000 of down-payment cash. At $584,000, down is $146,000 and total is $282,000, still $12,000 short. The remaining price reduction lowers the loan amount, not closing-day cash.

The arithmetic cash ceiling is ($270,000 − $136,000) ÷ 25% = $536,000. That is a $64,000 price reduction to remove the $16,000 funding gap, under these stipulated assumptions. At that ceiling, $134,000 down plus $136,000 fixed allocation uses all $270,000; the assigned $40,000 reserve remains, but no additional cushion is left. This is not a market valuation, an acceptable offer recommendation or a prediction the seller will agree.

Actual lender terms, appraisal, rate, insurance, tax and price-linked closing charges can change with the price and property condition; re-underwrite them rather than treating this illustration as universal. A repair credit or escrow arrangement is not automatically available cash: have lender, title and advisers verify the actual treatment. Lower debt payments may improve a separately modeled operating case, but cannot be counted as an additional closing-day cash contribution. Keep reserves once, and do not assume a tax refund or projected bookings close the gap.

Ask each team to distinguish cash feasibility from purchase value and income durability. Reprice only with supported evidence and actual approval; retain valid contract protection while decisive evidence is unresolved. Use the purchase cash planner. A financially infeasible candidate should not become acceptable merely because a platform estimate is attractive.

Which option fits your first purchase?

Buyers who want a sourced property, detailed underwriting, negotiation, and closing coordination. Confirm the exact deliverables, service territory, and engagement fee.

Investors who want to research opportunities and assemble their own agent, lender, inspector, and operator. The buyer must reconcile model estimates with the home's condition and expense base.

Choose the proposal that assigns responsibility, explains its model and fits your cash and time limits. Do not sign merely because a sales timeline or tax illustration looks attractive.
