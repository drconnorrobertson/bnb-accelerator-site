HostGPO pricing: fees, total cost and buyer FAQ

HostGPO presents a purchasing membership for vacation-rental hosts buying supplies, furnishings and other property products.

A HostGPO decision to resolve

Separate membership dues from merchandise, shipping, returns and installation.

What does HostGPO cost?

The reviewed pricing page displays a HostGPO membership at $149 per year. Product purchases, delivery and installation remain separate budget items. Confirm the selected membership and current terms.

What is being priced?

HostGPO presents a purchasing membership for vacation-rental hosts buying supplies, furnishings and other property products. Confirm the exact product, service tier, property count, billing period, currency, contract term and local availability.

Which costs belong in the comparison?

Include hardware, installation, subscriptions, labor, payment charges and replacement costs. Separate membership benefits and procurement savings from furnishing project delivery. Put recurring charges and one-time implementation costs on separate lines.

Is the advertised amount the entire property budget?

No. Distinguish a service charge or subscription from purchase capital and reserves. For a property purchase, separately budget down payment, closing costs, repairs, furnishings, opening costs and retained operating cash. For an operating product, include the labor and connected services needed to use it.

What should a written quote show?

Record the contracting entity, selected tier, tax and currency, quantity, due dates, minimum commitment, variable-fee base, cancellation rights, renewal and exclusions. Ask which charges apply when a property is vacant, onboarding is delayed or the service is terminated.

How can I compare the annual cost without inventing a vendor price?

Use the actual written quote. Annual service cost equals fixed recurring charges for the year, plus one-time setup, plus variable charges based on your expected usage, plus required third-party costs. Run a lower-usage and a higher-usage case. This is a budgeting method, not a quoted HostGPO fee.

OptionResponsibility to clarify
Fixed recurring chargesMultiply the quoted charge by the stated number of billing periods; verify quantity and currency.
Variable chargesApply the agreed rate to the explicitly defined revenue, transactions, listings or other base.
Setup and required extrasAdd onboarding, equipment, migration, installation or independent professional costs when applicable.
Cancellation and renewalDocument notice, remaining commitments, data access and the price after any promotion ends.

How should I compare this with BNB Accelerator pricing?

Compare the work delivered. BNB Accelerator publishes acquisition-scope guidance and requests a current written service proposal. Read BNB Accelerator pricing and scope, then separate any complementary property operations and service tools expense from the acquisition engagement. Do not replace a service quote with a property example or an income headline.
