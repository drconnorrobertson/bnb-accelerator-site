BNB Accelerator vs STR University: Which STR Program Is Right for You?

Published August 15, 2026·9 min read

STR University is a cohort-based training program for short-term rental hosts and investors, and BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you acquisition service. A cohort teaches you the work on a schedule alongside other students. We do the work. Which is right depends on whether the thing you are short of is knowledge or time.

The short answer

STR University is a cohort-based training. BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you short-term rental acquisition service for high-income earners. Cohort accountability and hands-on coverage of details like direct booking sites and guidebooks that broader programs often skip. We source, underwrite, negotiate, and launch a property you own outright, structured around a tax position from the start.

What STR University does

STR University runs courses in a cohort format, which means students move through material together on a set schedule rather than working through a library alone. Public descriptions emphasise practicality and applicability, with coverage that includes direct booking site setup and guest guidebook creation, areas that many broader programs skim.

The cohort structure is worth understanding, because it changes the experience. Accountability is higher than a self-paced course, questions get answered in context, and you build a peer group going through the same problems at the same time. That last part is underrated: most people who abandon a self-paced course do so because nothing external is holding them to it.

Confirm current curriculum, cohort schedule, and pricing directly with STR University, since cohort programs change format between intakes.

What BNB Accelerator does

We are a done-for-you acquisition service. Our team screens more than 1,000 listings a week across eight states and eliminates roughly 98% of them. What survives is underwritten line by line against comparable revenue, then presented to you with the full model attached. We write and negotiate the offer, coordinate inspection, appraisal, lender and title, and pair you with vetted local designers, furnishing crews, and property managers already operating in that market.

Two things make the engagement different from most of this category. First, we are paid by you, the buyer, which is what makes a recommendation to walk away possible. Second, the tax outcome drives the purchase rather than following it: price point, closing date, and management structure are set against what the deduction needs to accomplish, because a full-service management agreement signed in week one can quietly eliminate the deduction that justified the purchase.

Track record: more than 500 homes closed for over 260 clients across five-plus years, with roughly an 80% repeat buyer rate. See documented client results and what clients say.

The category difference matters more than the feature list

Most of the confusion in this space comes from comparing things that are not the same kind of thing. A course sells you knowledge. A data tool sells you information. A brokerage represents you in a transaction. A fund sells you a security. A turnkey provider sells you a finished product. We are an acquisition service: we do the work of finding, underwriting, negotiating, and closing a property that you own outright, and we structure it around a tax position first.

Decide which category you want before you compare providers inside it. That single decision eliminates most of the field.

When STR University is the better choice

We would rather tell you this plainly than have you find out after signing. Choose STR University if:

  • You learn better with structure, deadlines, and a peer group than from a video library.
  • You want to build the skill so you can repeat the process without paying anyone.
  • Direct booking and guest experience details are priorities for you.
  • You are earlier in the journey and want understanding before committing capital.

When BNB Accelerator is the better choice

  • You have capital ready and a tax year you are trying to affect, which makes a cohort schedule the wrong pace.
  • You want the acquisition underwritten by people who close in these markets rather than learning to underwrite it yourself.
  • You want design and management pairing handled rather than added to your list.
  • Repeatable skill is not the goal. The asset is.

Can you use both?

In most pairings on this page, yes. Data platforms, courses, and review sites all combine perfectly well with an acquisition service, and several of our clients use them together. The genuine conflicts are with other acquisition-side providers, where two parties would be performing the same role on the same purchase.

If you are weighing several options at once, the four questions that separate providers fastest are: who pays you, how many properties do you reject for every one you present, what happens if nothing meets my criteria this month, and who handles the tax structuring. Vague answers to any of those are informative.

What the decision actually costs

It is worth being concrete about why this choice matters, because the fee difference between options in this category is usually the smallest number involved.

On a $700,000 purchase, a market chosen without a proper regulatory check can become unrentable if a permit cap arrives, and the resale market for a short-term rental that can no longer operate short-term is the long-term rental market, which prices it very differently. Underwriting to a peak year rather than a trailing median commonly overstates revenue by 20 to 30 percent, which is the entire cash flow on a leveraged deal. Signing a full-service management agreement before speaking to a CPA can defeat material participation and forfeit a first-year deduction worth six figures to a high earner.

None of those are exotic failure modes. They are the three most common ways a short-term rental purchase goes wrong, and all three are decided before closing. Any course, tool, or service you are considering should be judged on how well it protects you from those three specifically. Where we are the wrong choice

We are wrong for you if your marginal tax rate is low, because the deduction that drives most of our clients' returns is worth proportionally less to you. We are wrong if you cannot materially participate and will not co-host, because the loss will suspend and you will carry short-term rental workload for long-term rental tax treatment. We are wrong if you want to learn this business yourself, because we will have done the interesting part for you. And we are wrong if you do not have capital ready for a down payment, furnishing, closing costs, and reserves, in which case education is the better spend and we would tell you so on the first call.

Frequently asked questions

Is BNB Accelerator better than STR University?

They are different categories. STR University teaches you to do this yourself in a cohort format with real accountability. BNB Accelerator does the acquisition for you. If your gap is knowledge, a cohort is the better purchase. If your gap is time, an acquisition service is.

What does STR University teach?

Public descriptions emphasise practical, applicable training for hosts and investors, including direct booking site setup and guidebook creation alongside core short-term rental material. Confirm the current curriculum and cohort schedule directly with them.

How much does STR University cost?

Pricing is not consistently published on the open web and varies by cohort and program tier. Confirm current pricing and intake dates directly with STR University.

Is a cohort better than a self-paced course?

For most people, yes, on completion rates. Deadlines and a peer group create accountability that self-paced libraries do not. The tradeoff is that you move at the cohort's pace rather than your own.

Can I take a cohort and still work with an acquisition service?

Yes. There is no conflict, and understanding the underwriting makes you a sharper reviewer of the deals we bring. It is a question of budget rather than compatibility.
