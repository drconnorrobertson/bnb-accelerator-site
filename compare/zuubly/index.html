Questions buyers ask

Is Zuubly a short-term rental investing company?

Based on publicly visible pages, no. It appears to operate as a review site publishing assessments of short-term rental courses and coaching programs, rather than offering investing, brokerage, or acquisition services itself.

Is BNB Accelerator better than Zuubly?

The comparison does not really apply, because they are different kinds of thing. Zuubly publishes reviews of education programs; BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you acquisition service. You might read a review site while deciding what help you want, then engage a service to execute.

Are review sites for STR courses trustworthy?

Treat them as a starting point. Many review sites in the education space earn affiliate commission on the programs they cover, which can influence coverage and tone. Look for disclosed affiliate relationships, cross-check against independent sources, and speak to actual past students before buying anything.

How should I evaluate an STR program or service?

Ask who pays the provider, what specifically is included, what happens if you are unsatisfied, and whether you can speak to recent clients. For an acquisition service, also ask how many properties they reject for every one they present, and what happens if nothing meets your criteria this month.

Does BNB Accelerator pay for reviews?

No. We would rather you read our own honest breakdown of who we are wrong for, speak to past clients, and check the Trustpilot reviews including the critical ones.
