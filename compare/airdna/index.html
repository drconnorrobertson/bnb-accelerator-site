Questions buyers ask

Is BNB Accelerator better than AirDNA?

They are not comparable products. AirDNA is a data subscription; BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you acquisition service. Asking which is better is like asking whether a spreadsheet is better than an accountant. Many of our clients subscribe to AirDNA and work with us at the same time.

How much does AirDNA cost?

Publicly reported pricing ranges from roughly $39.99 a month for entry-level market data up to $199 and above for professional tiers with comparable sets, custom dashboards, and API access. Confirm current pricing directly with AirDNA.

Is AirDNA accurate?

It is useful for ranking markets and unreliable for any individual property. Scraped calendar data cannot distinguish an owner-blocked night from a booked one, and it cannot judge whether a comparable is genuinely comparable. Use it to screen, not to make an offer.

Do I need AirDNA if I work with BNB Accelerator?

No, but you are welcome to keep it. Some clients use it to independently check the revenue assumptions in the models we send, which we think is a reasonable thing to do.

What data do you use instead?

Our own closing data across more than 500 homes, combined with hand-selected active comparables in each submarket: eight to twelve listings within one bedroom of the subject, live at least twelve months, underwritten to the median rather than the mean.
