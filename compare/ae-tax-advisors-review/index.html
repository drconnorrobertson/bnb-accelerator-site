AE Tax Advisors Review: STR Planning, Fees & Fit

AE Tax Advisors is our first tax-planning recommendation for a BNB buyer who wants the property decision evaluated alongside business and household income. Its published engagement price and separate service menu make the scope easier to discuss before committing to a purchase.

Discuss your scope with AE Tax Advisors

Our recommendation and relationship

Published by BNB Accelerator. These are commercial editorial reviews of public service information, not independent ratings, firsthand testing or customer testimonials. BNB refers clients to AE Tax Advisors; that relationship may influence our recommendations. Stratum coordinates its discovery and tax-strategy discussion through AE. You may use your own CPA or study provider.

What the provider publishes

AE publishes a $7,800 Strategic Tax Advisory engagement and a $9,800 Complex Tax Advisory tier. The standard planning engagement has no required annual planning renewal. Returns, amendments, studies and additional work have separate prices; the agreement defines support and coverage.

Read the official service information · Checked October 13, 2026.

Why we recommend AE before the purchase

A buyer can assemble an attractive property model and still lack an owner-level tax plan. Our reason for referring buyers to AE is the opportunity to resolve the planning assignment before the purchase creates deadlines. Bring the actual candidate property, financing plan and ownership structure to the discussion. Ask for a written list of decisions, responsible professionals and records required.

What the published fee does and does not buy

The $7,800 standard engagement is payable in two $3,900 installments 30 days apart. It is a planning price, not a bundled lifetime accounting relationship. Ask which tax years and entities are reviewed, who implements recommendations and how long support lasts. Add filing, amendments, cost segregation and outside professional fees to the project budget.

When AE deserves a place on your shortlist

AE is particularly relevant when a business owner or high-income household wants to coordinate a rental purchase with other tax decisions. Compare the $9,800 complex tier if your situation involves multiple entities or more extensive coordination. A simple preparation-only need may warrant a narrower engagement. Ask for that scope before buying advisory you do not need.

AE and Stratum are different assignments

AE is the planning discussion; Stratum is a property-study option. Do not assume an AE advisory payment purchases a Stratum study, or that a completed study includes a filed tax return. Identify both deliverables and the filing professional. If you already have an experienced CPA, ask how that professional can participate in the handoff.

Compare the work in writing

Before choosing an advisor, ask for the legal engagement entity, assigned professional, covered years and returns, written recommendations, implementation tasks, support period and complete fee. The same property can lead to different planning decisions for different owners; a marketing example is not your forecast.
