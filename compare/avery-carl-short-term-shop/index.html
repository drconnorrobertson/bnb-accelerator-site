BNB Accelerator vs The Short Term Shop: Which Is Right for You?

Published August 15, 2026·9 min read

The Short Term Shop is a short-term-rental-specialised real estate brokerage founded by Avery Carl, and BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you acquisition service. Both help you buy a short-term rental, and they are structurally different: a brokerage represents you as your agent in a transaction, while we run the sourcing, underwriting, negotiation, and launch around a tax position you define first.

The short answer

Avery Carl / The Short Term Shop is a real estate brokerage. BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you short-term rental acquisition service for high-income earners. Specialist agent representation at genuine scale, in markets they transact in daily, at no direct cost to the buyer. We source, underwrite, negotiate, and launch a property you own outright, structured around a tax position from the start.

What The Short Term Shop does

The Short Term Shop is a real estate sales team founded by Avery Carl, operating under eXp Realty and specialising exclusively in short-term rental purchases. It is, by its own account, the largest short-term-rental-specific brokerage in the United States, with more than $4 billion in vacation rental transactions closed, 75-plus agents across 20-plus markets, and three-time recognition as the number one team worldwide at eXp Realty.

Avery Carl herself is a Wall Street Journal Top 100 agent who built a personal portfolio of more than 250 doors, wrote two bestselling books, Short-Term Rental, Long-Term Wealth and Smarter Short-Term Rentals, and hosts The Short Term Show podcast. She also co-founded The Mortgage Shop, which gives the operation a financing arm alongside the brokerage.

What you get is agent representation from people who transact in short-term rental markets every day rather than a generalist who sells three vacation properties a year. That is a meaningful edge over a typical buyer's agent, and the volume behind it is real.

What BNB Accelerator does

We are a done-for-you acquisition service. Our team screens more than 1,000 listings a week across eight states and eliminates roughly 98% of them. What survives is underwritten line by line against comparable revenue, then presented to you with the full model attached. We write and negotiate the offer, coordinate inspection, appraisal, lender and title, and pair you with vetted local designers, furnishing crews, and property managers already operating in that market.

Two things make the engagement different from most of this category. First, we are paid by you, the buyer, which is what makes a recommendation to walk away possible. Second, the tax outcome drives the purchase rather than following it: price point, closing date, and management structure are set against what the deduction needs to accomplish, because a full-service management agreement signed in week one can quietly eliminate the deduction that justified the purchase.

Track record: more than 500 homes closed for over 260 clients across five-plus years, with roughly an 80% repeat buyer rate. See documented client results and what clients say.

The category difference matters more than the feature list

Most of the confusion in this space comes from comparing things that are not the same kind of thing. A course sells you knowledge. A data tool sells you information. A brokerage represents you in a transaction. A fund sells you a security. A turnkey provider sells you a finished product. We are an acquisition service: we do the work of finding, underwriting, negotiating, and closing a property that you own outright, and we structure it around a tax position first.

Decide which category you want before you compare providers inside it. That single decision eliminates most of the field.

When The Short Term Shop is the better choice

We would rather tell you this plainly than have you find out after signing. Choose The Short Term Shop if:

  • You want representation in a market we do not operate in. Their footprint is much wider than ours.
  • You are comfortable running your own underwriting and want a specialist agent to execute the transaction.
  • You already have a CPA who understands material participation and cost segregation, so the tax layer is handled.
  • You would rather not pay a separate engagement fee and are happy with the standard commission structure.

When BNB Accelerator is the better choice

  • Your primary objective is offsetting a large W-2 or business tax bill, and the purchase needs to be structured around that from the start.
  • You want the underwriting done adversarially by someone whose incentive is not tied to the deal closing.
  • You want furnishing, design, and management pairing handled rather than coordinated by you after closing.
  • You want a small number of properties bought well rather than a wide market search.

Can you use both?

In most pairings on this page, yes. Data platforms, courses, and review sites all combine perfectly well with an acquisition service, and several of our clients use them together. The genuine conflicts are with other acquisition-side providers, where two parties would be performing the same role on the same purchase.

If you are weighing several options at once, the four questions that separate providers fastest are: who pays you, how many properties do you reject for every one you present, what happens if nothing meets my criteria this month, and who handles the tax structuring. Vague answers to any of those are informative.

What the decision actually costs

It is worth being concrete about why this choice matters, because the fee difference between options in this category is usually the smallest number involved.

On a $700,000 purchase, a market chosen without a proper regulatory check can become unrentable if a permit cap arrives, and the resale market for a short-term rental that can no longer operate short-term is the long-term rental market, which prices it very differently. Underwriting to a peak year rather than a trailing median commonly overstates revenue by 20 to 30 percent, which is the entire cash flow on a leveraged deal. Signing a full-service management agreement before speaking to a CPA can defeat material participation and forfeit a first-year deduction worth six figures to a high earner.

None of those are exotic failure modes. They are the three most common ways a short-term rental purchase goes wrong, and all three are decided before closing. Any course, tool, or service you are considering should be judged on how well it protects you from those three specifically. See the mistakes that cost the most and how to check regulations before buying.

Where we are the wrong choice

We are wrong for you if your marginal tax rate is low, because the deduction that drives most of our clients' returns is worth proportionally less to you. We are wrong if you cannot materially participate and will not co-host, because the loss will suspend and you will carry short-term rental workload for long-term rental tax treatment. We are wrong if you want to learn this business yourself, because we will have done the interesting part for you. And we are wrong if you do not have capital ready for a down payment, furnishing, closing costs, and reserves, in which case education is the better spend and we would tell you so on the first call.

Frequently asked questions

Is BNB Accelerator better than The Short Term Shop?

Neither is better in the abstract, because they are different services. The Short Term Shop is a specialist brokerage that represents you in a transaction across 20-plus markets. BNB Accelerator is a done-for-you acquisition service that underwrites, negotiates, and launches the property around a tax position. If you want breadth of market coverage and agent representation, they are the stronger fit. If you want the work done for you and the purchase structured around a deduction, we are.

Does The Short Term Shop cost anything?

Brokerage services are customarily compensated through the transaction commission, typically from the seller side, so buyers generally do not pay a separate fee. Verify the current arrangement directly with them. Our engagement is paid by the buyer, which is what allows us to be paid for telling you not to buy something.

Who is Avery Carl?

Avery Carl is the founder and CEO of The Short Term Shop, a Wall Street Journal Top 100 agent, and the author of Short-Term Rental, Long-Term Wealth and Smarter Short-Term Rentals. She built a personal portfolio of more than 250 doors and hosts The Short Term Show podcast.

Can I use both The Short Term Shop and BNB Accelerator?

Not on the same property, since both would be performing the acquisition role. Some investors use a specialist brokerage in markets outside our eight states and work with us where we operate. There is no conflict in that arrangement.

Which one is better for a first short-term rental?

It depends on what you want to learn. Working with a specialist agent leaves more of the analysis with you, which is valuable if you intend to build the skill. A done-for-you acquisition removes most of that learning curve, which is the point if your constraint is time rather than interest.
