Run this decision exercise before an offer

Ask both providers to respond to the same rejected-property scenario: a matched address fails the inspection or insurance check. Record who updates the model, finds another candidate, advises the offer decision and tracks the deadline. The answer should identify a person and a contractual obligation rather than just a list of partners.

Buyer fit: compare the next obligation, not just the first match

For a buyer purchasing in the next six months, the useful distinction is which missing work the signed engagement fills. BNB Turnkey's services page describes market selection, forecasting, virtual tours, inspection and negotiation support, design/furnishing management and budget oversight. That makes it a relevant proposal for broader coordination. It does not authenticate your package, a fixed launch price or any obligation to replace a rejected property.

STR Search's matching page emphasizes screening data, market saturation, revenue and amenities. Its main site discusses a separate realtor role and disclaims guaranteed financial performance. Evaluate the actual matching tier if your gap is candidate selection and analysis. Neither a marketing match promise nor a forecast establishes lender approval, insurability, permit eligibility or a contractual refund. This comparison is document-based analysis, not firsthand testing, a customer rating or verification of either firm's results.

Broader is not automatically better. An investor with an established agent, contractor and manager may already have execution coverage; another buyer may need a named coordinator to reconcile those parties. Ask each firm to identify supplied work, exclusions and the actual decision-maker. Treat BNB Accelerator's proposal the same way: published positioning is not a substitute for signed scope.

Ask for the replacement-search clock in writing: when it starts, whether rejected candidates consume it, and what happens if a replacement arrives after the service period or your financing deadline. A thirty-day marketing message is not evidence that a rejected match restarts the clock. Do not infer a particular remedy without the actual terms. Worked purchase cash: a replacement can cost money even without another service invoice

Hypothetical arithmetic only, not either provider's fee, a client result or a refund promise: assume $320,000 available. A replacement property's complete plan assigns $165,000 down payment, $18,000 distinct closing/service costs, $38,000 setup, $19,000 repairs and $50,000 retained operating cash. Allocation is $290,000, leaving $30,000 outside the plan. This plan excludes all costs of the rejected candidate. Replace the invented figures with dated quotes; the retained cash is assigned once, not spent again to make a gap disappear.

Suppose the rejected candidate separately consumed $1,200 in inspection, $800 in appraisal and $2,500 in a nonrefundable vendor commitment: $4,500 total. Those invented losses are not included in the replacement's $290,000. Combined cash use is $294,500 and remaining margin is $25,500. If the actual contract requires an additional $6,000 replacement-search invoice, cash use becomes $300,500 and margin $19,500. If replacement work is already included, do not add that invoice; the margin remains $25,500. Neither treatment is a published provider quote.

A separate $12,000 repair discovered on the replacement reduces those margins to $7,500 with the incremental invoice, or $13,500 without it. An expected $3,000 credit restricted to a future service is not cleared purchase cash and does not increase either margin. If an actual $3,000 cash refund clears before the relevant payment deadline, recalculate from the documented receipt—not from the promise. This exercise excludes any additional holding/delay costs, taxes or other losses; add actual obligations without counting them twice. It establishes neither profit nor financing qualification.

Fair alternatives and the next purchase decision

If neither proposal fills the missing role, compare STR Insights' actual service tier for analysis/support or The Short Term Shop's local representation and education proposal. These are fair alternatives to investigate, not interchangeable packages or declared winners. BNB Accelerator is the interested publisher; request its current contracted acquisition scope, the same rejected-candidate work sample and its exclusions. Keep buyer authority, lender/insurance decisions and professional tax/legal advice separate from provider recommendations.

Before choosing, complete the register against one real candidate and the purchase cash worksheet. Then discuss the acquisition responsibilities you need covered. Use the comparison hub to follow the existing canonical reviews rather than treat additional page count as additional evidence.

Compare evidence using one actual candidate

Ask for a redacted decision file for a property similar to your target. It should separate source records from estimates, explain rejected comparables and show the purchase cash needed when revenue is below the base case. Obtain current lender and insurance quotes rather than assuming an advertised rate or projected revenue will qualify the deal. Confirm the legal-use question for the exact parcel with the appropriate local authority and advisers.

A contract-to-close responsibility map

Put a name and due date next to representation, inspections, title, financing, permit questions and the closing funds schedule. Mark introductions separately from completed work. Before a contingency expires, record what has been verified, what remains uncertain and who has authority to accept the risk. The strongest fit is the proposal that covers your actual gaps with documented deliverables and a complete budget.

Questions before committing

How should a buyer choose between BNB Turnkey and STR Search?

Do you need a property match or broader purchase coordination? Use the written deliverables, intended ownership rights and the work your purchase team still needs.

Can I compare BNB Turnkey and STR Search using advertised returns?

First align ownership, rental strategy, expenses, financing and decision authority. Advertised projections are estimates and should not replace address-specific records and a downside case.

What should I request before signing with BNB Turnkey or STR Search?

Request current fees, included and excluded tasks, named contracting entities, cancellation terms and a sample address decision file. Resolve the key purchase question: Do you need a property match or broader purchase coordination?

Does this comparison establish a current price for BNB Turnkey or STR Search?

No. Request a current written quote for the exact product, market and property scope. Compare total purchase cash separately from provider fees.
