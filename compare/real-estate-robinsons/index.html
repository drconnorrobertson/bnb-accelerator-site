Questions buyers ask

Is BNB Accelerator better than The Real Estate Robinsons?

They are different products for different situations. The Robinsons teach short-term rental investing through coaching, a bootcamp, and a live summit, and they are genuinely strong for beginners. BNB Accelerator does the acquisition for you. If you want to learn, take their program. If you want the asset without the learning curve, use an acquisition service.

Who are Tony and Sara Robinson?

They are a husband-and-wife short-term rental investing team who built a multi-million-dollar portfolio and now run an education business including a bootcamp, the three-day STR Summit event, and the Alpha Host coaching program. Tony co-hosts the BiggerPockets Real Estate Rookie podcast.

What is the STR Summit?

It is a three-day live event they host, aimed at helping newer investors buy, manage, and scale a short-term rental portfolio. Confirm current dates, format, and pricing directly with them.

Do I need coaching before buying a short-term rental?

Not if you are using an acquisition service, since the analysis is done for you. You do need coaching or self-study if you intend to source and underwrite yourself, because the acquisition decision is the one that is hardest to reverse.

Can I do both?

Yes. Some clients take a program to understand the mechanics and use an acquisition service to execute. Understanding underwriting makes you a better reviewer of the deals we present.
