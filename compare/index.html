The Honest Version

Which one is actually right for you

Option 1

Do it yourself

Choose this if: you have 150 to 300 hours available over the next six months, you genuinely enjoy market research and deal analysis, and you want to build the skill permanently.

What it costs you: time, and risk concentrated in the things you cannot see. An HOA rental cap buried on page 40 of the covenants. A municipality about to restrict permits. A revenue projection built on the three best months of the year. One bad purchase costs more than the fee on ten good ones.

The honest math: if your time is worth $200 an hour or more, 200 hours is $40,000 of opportunity cost before you have made a single offer. See the full breakdown.

Option 2

BNB Mastery

What it is: an education and coaching business associated with James Svetec, focused on short-term rental operations. Historically much of the content has centered on rental arbitrage and management rather than property ownership.

Choose this if: you have more time than capital, you want to operate rather than own, or you want to build the skill set yourself with structured coaching support.

Why it is different from us: you learn a system and then execute it. We do not sell a system. Arbitrage in particular is a fundamentally different business from ownership, and notably it does not produce the depreciation and tax offset that motivates most of our clients, because you do not own the asset.

Option 3

Robuilt

What it is: an education and community business associated with Rob Abasolo, focused on short-term rental investing, unique-stay builds like A-frames and glamping, and self-management.

Choose this if: you are drawn to the creative and build side of short-term rentals, you want a community of investors, and you have the time and appetite to run projects yourself.

Why it is different from us: the unique-stay and ground-up build strategy is capital-intensive, timeline-heavy, and operationally demanding in ways that do not fit a surgeon working sixty hours a week. We buy existing inventory with proven booking history precisely because that is what a busy professional can absorb.

Option 4

BNB Accelerator

What it is: an acquisition team working on your side of the table. We source, underwrite, negotiate, and close properties you own directly, then pair you with vetted design and management vendors.

Choose this if: you earn $500,000 or more, you are losing six figures a year to taxes, you have capital and no time, and you want the asset without acquiring the skill set.

The tradeoff to be clear about: you pay a fee and you do not become a short-term rental expert. If either of those bothers you, one of the options above is a better fit and we would rather you pick it. See exactly what we do.

Two other models worth understanding

Turnkey providers

These sell you a finished, furnished, sometimes already-operating property out of their own inventory. It is fast, and for some buyers that speed is worth it. The structural problem is the incentive: they profit from the sale price, which means the person telling you the property is worth $850,000 is the same person receiving $850,000. Ask any provider a single question -- do you make more money when I pay more for the property? Our answer is no, because we do not own inventory. That is the entire reason we can negotiate against a seller on your behalf.

Syndications and funds

These give you passive exposure to a portfolio someone else assembles and operates. Genuinely hands-off, genuinely diversified, and a reasonable product for the right investor. The catch for our client profile is that you own an interest in an entity rather than the real estate itself, which generally means you do not get the short-term rental tax treatment that motivates most people reading this page. You also do not control the asset, the exit, or the timing. If the tax offset is your primary objective, direct ownership is usually the only structure that delivers it.
