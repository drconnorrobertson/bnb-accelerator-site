Why we work with an outside tax firm

My BnB Accelerator is a real estate acquisition firm. We source, underwrite, negotiate, and close short-term rental properties. We are not a CPA firm, we do not prepare returns, and we do not give tax advice.

That matters because the strategy our clients pursue is substantially a tax strategy. Buying the right property is necessary and not sufficient: the deduction only materialises if the seven day average stay test is met, if material participation is genuinely established and documented, and if a cost segregation study is performed properly. Those are all questions for a qualified professional.

So we refer clients to an independent firm rather than pretending to competence we do not have.

The nature of the relationship

AE Tax Advisors is an independent firm. It is not owned by, controlled by, or affiliated with My BnB Accelerator, LLC beyond a referral relationship. You are under no obligation to use them, and clients frequently use their own CPA instead, which is entirely fine with us.

We introduce them because the tax questions in this strategy are specific and get answered badly by generalists. If your existing CPA has short-term rental experience and understands material participation and cost segregation, keep them.

Nothing on this site is tax, legal, or investment advice. Tax outcomes depend entirely on your individual facts and circumstances. Consult your own CPA or attorney before acting.

Questions worth asking any tax advisor

  1. How many short-term rental clients do you work with, and how many cost segregation studies have you overseen?
  2. How do you document material participation, and what does the log you expect from me look like?
  3. How does my state treat bonus depreciation, and does it conform to the federal treatment?
  4. What happens to this position when I sell, and how should the exit be timed?
  5. What is the excess business loss limitation likely to do to my deduction this year?

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is AE Tax Advisors owned by BNB Accelerator?

No. AE Tax Advisors is an independent firm and is not owned by, controlled by, or affiliated with My BnB Accelerator, LLC beyond a referral relationship. Clients are under no obligation to use them.

Do I have to use your partner tax firm?

No. Many clients use their own CPA, which is entirely fine. We introduce AE Tax Advisors because the tax questions in this strategy are specific and often get answered badly by generalists. If your CPA already understands material participation and cost segregation, keep them.

Does BNB Accelerator give tax advice?

No. We are a real estate acquisition firm. We source, underwrite, negotiate, and close properties. We do not prepare returns, perform cost segregation studies, or advise on tax positions.

Who performs the cost segregation study?

A qualified tax or engineering firm, not us. AE Tax Advisors oversees studies for many of our clients. You are free to use any provider; see our guide on choosing a cost segregation firm for the questions worth asking.
