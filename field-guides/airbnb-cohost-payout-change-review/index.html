Review an Airbnb Co-Host Payout Change

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Changing an agreement with a co-host and changing Airbnb’s payout settings are separate actions. Airbnb requires the listing owner to set up the proposal and the co-host to confirm it, with timing rules for when changes affect bookings. A payout worksheet connects the agreed arrangement to the platform record without assuming that a message or verbal agreement has already changed payments.

Describe the agreed calculation

Record whether the proposed payout is a cleaning-fee amount, a percentage, a combined option or a fixed amount available in the account. State whether the percentage includes the cleaning fee. Do not use a phrase such as the usual share without a defined basis. Keep the service agreement and payout configuration aligned while recognizing they serve different purposes.

Confirm authority and eligibility

Airbnb says only the listing owner can set up or edit these payout arrangements. Verify applicable regional or program limitations and the co-host’s required account setup. A co-host permission level does not itself establish payout eligibility. Do not share credentials so another person can impersonate the owner to configure a payment.

Track proposal and acceptance dates

Record when the proposal was sent and when it was accepted, declined or replaced. Airbnb’s guidance gives the co-host a confirmation window and ties changes to bookings starting after confirmation. Use the actual account record to determine affected bookings. A backdated spreadsheet entry does not change the platform’s effective timing.

Use a transition example

Suppose a new percentage is agreed while one guest is already staying and another arrives next week. Review each reservation against the confirmed change date rather than applying the new share to all payouts received afterward. If a separate settlement is needed under the parties’ agreement, document it independently and avoid duplicating amounts the platform already distributed.

Exclude unsupported payout categories

Airbnb states that this sharing feature covers home-stay payouts and does not include certain other payments, such as Resolution Center payouts or host-damage reimbursements. Identify any amount the parties expect to share outside that feature. Do not assume a percentage setting automatically divides every payment or resolves the parties’ accounting and tax obligations.

Review the first affected booking

Compare the expected calculation with the displayed payout for an actual eligible reservation. Check cleaning-fee treatment and any adjustment affecting the amount. Keep the proposal, acceptance and reconciliation together. If the result differs, investigate before issuing a separate payment. The worksheet verifies execution of a specific arrangement without promising earnings or replacing professional accounting advice. Record who reviewed the first calculation and when, so an unresolved difference is not silently carried into the next group of reservations.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Agreed payout option and basis
  • Owner authority and account eligibility
  • Proposal and acceptance timestamps
  • Affected reservation start dates
  • Excluded payment categories
  • First-booking reconciliation
