Check an Airbnb Special Offer Before Sending

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

An Airbnb special offer can change a proposed price, dates or listing before a guest books. It is not a casual estimate: Airbnb says a guest’s acceptance automatically confirms the reservation. Review the offer as a booking decision with a complete price and availability check. A short worksheet helps prevent a discount conversation from becoming an incorrect confirmed stay.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Confirm that the workflow applies

Airbnb’s special-offer guidance applies before the guest has booked or submitted a trip request. If the reservation is already in another state, use the applicable change process instead. Record the current message and booking status first. Do not force a special offer into an existing booking simply because it seems faster than reviewing the proper workflow.

Check dates and listing identity

Verify the exact property, arrival, departure and guest details that the offer will use. Recheck the calendar and any pending requests affecting the dates. If another listing is proposed, ensure the guest understands the substitution. An internal nickname or copied message should not be the only evidence identifying which property will be booked.

Build the entire-stay subtotal

Airbnb directs hosts to include all nights and additional charges such as cleaning in the offer subtotal, with the platform handling other applicable components as described in its guidance. Reconcile the intended subtotal from a written breakdown before entering it. Avoid treating a nightly figure as the total stay price or adding an already included cleaning charge twice.

Use a pricing example

Suppose a guest asks for a discount on a four-night stay. List each night’s intended amount and the applicable included charges, then calculate the whole-stay offer. Compare that worksheet with the preview shown by Airbnb. This method checks arithmetic and scope; it does not justify changing disclosed mandatory fees or promising a specific guest total without reviewing the platform display.

Review acceptance and revisions

The current guidance gives guests a limited acceptance window and says acceptance confirms the stay. Verify the displayed deadline before sending. If the proposed terms change, use the supported withdrawal and replacement process rather than assuming a later message overrides an outstanding offer. Keep the old and new offer references so the team knows which version remains active.

Confirm the resulting booking

After acceptance, verify the reservation details and price in the account. Tell the operating team about any agreed condition that affects arrival or service delivery. If the offer expires or is withdrawn, record that outcome and recheck availability before sending another. Do not leave an informal worksheet marked confirmed when the platform never reached that state.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Applicable prebooking status
  • Exact listing and stay dates
  • Whole-stay subtotal breakdown
  • Offer preview and deadline
  • Withdrawal or replacement history
  • Final reservation details
