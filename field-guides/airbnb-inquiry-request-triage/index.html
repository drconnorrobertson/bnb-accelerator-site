Triage an Airbnb Inquiry or Reservation Request

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

An Airbnb inquiry and a reservation request are different states. An inquiry may ask about dates or property details without creating the same acceptance workflow as a request to book. A shared inbox should preserve that distinction so staff do not mistake a helpful message for a confirmed reservation or miss a required response. Start with the actual platform status, not the subject line of an email.

Identify the message state

Record the listing, guest, requested dates and whether the account shows an inquiry, reservation request or confirmed stay. Airbnb describes an inquiry as a message before a booking request. Do not classify it from the guest’s wording alone. Someone saying they want to book does not necessarily mean the platform has received a formal request.

Assign a response owner

Give the message a named owner and backup, especially when several co-hosts share an inbox. Record the next required action and the displayed deadline where applicable. Airbnb’s guidance distinguishes nonexpiring inquiries from reservation requests requiring an official response. Do not assume a conversational reply completes an accept-or-decline action that the interface still shows as pending.

Answer from verified property information

Confirm the relevant amenity, availability or rule before responding. If the answer is unknown, identify who can verify it and avoid inventing certainty to accelerate a booking. Keep prebooking communication on the platform under the applicable rules. Do not request unnecessary sensitive information merely to decide who should handle a routine question.

Use a date-question example

Suppose a traveler asks whether early arrival is possible. The operator records an inquiry and checks turnover constraints before answering. If a formal booking request later arrives, the team reviews it as a separate platform action. A favorable answer about early arrival is not treated as a confirmed booking or an unconditional promise before the operating schedule is known.

Separate availability from a promise to hold

Check the current calendar when discussing dates and explain any limits accurately. Do not tell several guests the same dates are reserved for them if the account has not created that status. If using a pre-approval or special offer, review the separate workflow and terms. Record the action actually sent rather than grouping all messages under approved.

Close the inbox task clearly

Save the answer, final platform status and any unresolved condition. Hand off upcoming deadlines when a shift changes. Review cases where a message was answered but a required platform action remained open, and adjust the checklist accordingly. The goal is reliable classification and follow-through, not a claim that faster responses guarantee bookings or improved ranking.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Actual inquiry/request/booking status
  • Named owner and backup
  • Displayed action or response deadline
  • Verified answer and open questions
  • Any offer or pre-approval action
  • Final status and shift handoff
