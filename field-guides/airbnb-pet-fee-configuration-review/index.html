Review Airbnb Pet-Fee Configuration and Guest Quotes

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

An ordinary-pet fee should be configured and disclosed through the platform’s permitted process, not improvised at check-in. Airbnb supports different pet-fee calculation bases and separately addresses service animals and emotional support animals. This worksheet concerns the price setup for ordinary pets. It does not decide whether a particular animal qualifies for an exception or override the platform’s current accessibility rules.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Write the intended price basis

Record whether the proposed fee is per booking, pet, night or pet per night, using options actually available in the account. Specify the permitted pet count and the relevant listing settings. A verbal instruction such as charge the usual dog fee is too ambiguous for another operator to implement consistently across stays of different lengths.

Check the current platform rule

Airbnb’s additional-fee guidance describes pet-fee options and warns against prohibited charges involving service animals and emotional support animals in applicable jurisdictions. Route those questions through the current policy and qualified guidance where needed. Do not classify an assistance animal as an ordinary pet simply to make the fee tool easier to use.

Test representative guest totals

Review the guest-facing price for a short stay and a longer stay with the applicable number of pets. Confirm that the fee behaves as intended and is disclosed before booking. Record dates, pet count, fee basis and displayed total. Avoid presenting a single test quote as proof that every date, promotion and guest combination will behave identically.

Resolve a changed booking through Airbnb

If an ordinary pet is added after booking, use the permitted reservation-change process and confirm the resulting amount with the guest. Do not collect an undisclosed mandatory surcharge through a personal payment account. If the account does not display the expected fee, investigate the configuration with support rather than substituting an informal cash request.

Use a calculation example carefully

Suppose a listing switches from a per-booking basis to a per-night basis. Review how that changes both a weekend and a week-long guest quote before saving the new setting. Keep the old and new configuration dates. The example is a test method, not a recommendation for a particular price or an assertion that higher charges improve returns.

Give the operating team a clear handoff

Record the approved setting, the person allowed to change it and the path for exception questions. Keep cleaning expectations separate from pet eligibility and from any damage claim. Review an actual completed booking against the quoted fee to spot configuration errors. The goal is consistent disclosure and accurate records without surprising guests or overriding applicable protections. Keep any approved change with the test results so a later operator can distinguish a deliberate pricing decision from a configuration mistake.

  • Current platform exception reference
  • Short and longer-stay quote checks
  • Reservation-change confirmation
