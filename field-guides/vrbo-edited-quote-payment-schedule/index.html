Review a Vrbo Edited Quote and Payment Schedule

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Editing a Vrbo quote can change more than the nightly amount. Dates, fees, taxes and payment timing can all matter, and the platform limits when a quote can be edited. Review the proposed revision before sending it so a guest receives a complete, internally consistent offer. This worksheet focuses on a prepayment quote, not an already-paid reservation amendment.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Verify the current booking stage

Vrbo’s quote guidance says a quote can be edited before the guest makes a payment. Confirm the actual status and payment history in the dashboard. If money has already been paid, use the appropriate reservation or support process instead. Do not rely on an old message thread that still displays a quote after the booking has advanced.

Rebuild the proposed total

Record dates, nightly amounts, applicable fees, taxes and any protection item shown. Compare the old and new quote line by line. Tax settings should reflect the actual responsible guidance, not an improvised adjustment to reach a desired total. Preserve the original quote so a later reviewer can see exactly which components changed.

Check extended-stay discounts deliberately

Vrbo explains that weekly or monthly discounts are not automatically applied when a quote is edited. If an agreed discount should remain, review and apply the appropriate rental amount manually through the supported process. Do not assume the final total preserves the earlier discount simply because the guest’s stay still qualifies in a general listing setting.

Review installment constraints

The current guidance says payment scheduling can depend on the account’s terms and the time before arrival. It also describes limits on changing a one-installment arrangement to multiple installments. Check the actual options shown rather than promising a payment plan in a message first. Record due amounts and dates from the final preview.

Use a longer-stay example

Suppose a guest extends a proposed stay before paying and expects the discussed discount to continue. Recalculate the rental amount, confirm fees and review the payment schedule. Send the revised quote with a clear explanation of the change. The guest’s interest in an extension does not establish that the revised quote has been accepted or paid.

Confirm the sent version and status

Save the final quote reference, totals, schedule and message. Under Vrbo’s pay-per-booking model, the guidance notes that quotes include a payment request, so verify the resulting status. If another revision is needed, identify which version remains current. Close the worksheet when the booking proceeds or the quote ends, without treating tentative interest as confirmed revenue. Let the next inbox operator know that a revised quote is outstanding, including its reference, so a second staff member does not send conflicting terms.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Prepayment status and original quote
  • Revised dates and line-item total
  • Extended-stay discount review
  • Installment amounts and due dates
  • Sent quote reference and message
  • Final acceptance/payment status
