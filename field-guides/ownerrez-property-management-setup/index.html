Software evaluation and operating scope

Scope OwnerRez Property-Management Accounting Setup

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

OwnerRez’s property-management documentation covers owner settings, commissions, expenses and statement behavior. These tools can support a manager’s accounting workflow, but the configuration must match the agreements and records behind it. A property owner buying software for personal use has different needs from a manager reporting to several clients. Do not equate a software module with outsourced management or accounting services.

Identify the parties and money flow

Record the property owner, manager and party receiving booking funds. Describe who pays expenses and how the owner is paid or invoiced. A setup cannot be evaluated from property count alone. The software should reflect the real arrangement rather than a convenient default that produces an attractive statement.

Translate the agreement into review questions

List commission bases, expense treatment and any booking-specific exceptions that require configuration. Ask the responsible accounting or legal adviser to resolve ambiguity in the agreement. Software support can explain field behavior, but should not be expected to interpret a disputed contract. Keep the approved interpretation with the setup record.

Review timing and deposit choices

The documentation distinguishes payment-related states used for owner statements. Decide which events the report is intended to reflect and confirm how the chosen setting works. Do not assume that a received payment and money deposited in a bank are identical. Match the report definition to the reconciliation process used by the business.

Test representative transactions

Use permitted test methods or reviewed historical examples to examine an ordinary booking, an expense and an exception. Check the commission and owner result by line item. Avoid changing live records merely to see what happens. Record the expected calculation and investigate differences before the first statement is issued.

Control owner-setting changes

Assign responsibility for changing commission or expense settings. OwnerRez describes copying some settings between owners, but that does not make every agreement identical. Review the applicable fields before applying a template broadly. Keep a dated record of authorized changes so later statements can be explained.

Use an exception example

Suppose one booking has a different commission arrangement under the written agreement. Confirm the appropriate booking-level treatment and review the resulting statement. Do not alter a global setting to solve one exception without checking other bookings. Preserve the supporting authorization and the final calculation for the accountant and owner. Include one report recipient in the acceptance review so the team knows whether the statement explains the agreed result. A technically correct formula can still be confusing when labels do not match the contract. Record any terminology change and retain the supporting calculation rather than changing the label without reviewing its financial meaning.
