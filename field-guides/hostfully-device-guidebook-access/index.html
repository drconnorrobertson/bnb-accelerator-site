Scope Guest Device Access Through Hostfully Guidebooks

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Hostfully documents a workflow linking Digital Guidebooks, its property-management platform and Hostfully Devices. The described guest-device access depends on those components and their configuration. A standalone guidebook should not be assumed to provide every connected function. Review the exact setup and test the authorized guest journey before using it for a live arrival.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

List the required components

Record the guidebook account, property-management account, device service and hardware involved. Identify who controls each one. Use Hostfully’s current integration instructions to check the required connections. Do not reduce a multi-product workflow to a statement that the guidebook alone controls the property’s devices.

Verify supported hardware and access

Ask the vendor about the actual devices and configuration you plan to use. A product category in a demonstration does not establish support for every model or installation. Keep hardware compatibility and professional installation questions separate from software setup. Confirm any additional subscriptions or service requirements in the proposal.

Understand the guest-specific link

The documentation describes generating a secure guidebook link for a confirmed booking when the relevant option is enabled. Ask how the link is created, delivered and limited for the actual setup. Do not paste a private guest link into public instructions or use one guest’s access to test another reservation.

Test through an approved process

Use the vendor’s supported method to verify the guest view and intended device actions. Check the relevant timing and what the guest sees when a connection is unavailable. Avoid manipulating real guest reservations without authorization. Record the test conditions so a successful demonstration is not mistaken for proof of every possible scenario.

Assign exception handling

Define who helps a guest when the link or device control fails and how an alternative access arrangement is approved. A digital guidebook is not a substitute for an operational backup. Keep support contacts and responsibilities current when the manager or device provider changes.

Review a device replacement

Suppose a lock is replaced with a different model. Recheck compatibility, account linking and the guest-facing workflow before relying on it. Update the device record and retain the verification result. An unchanged guidebook page does not establish that the new hardware is integrated correctly. Include a scenario in which the guest can open the guidebook but the device does not respond. Ask which provider supports that problem and what the onsite team should do. Keep that answer separate from a successful website demonstration, because access to information and control of physical equipment are different parts of the workflow.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Product and account dependency map
  • Exact hardware support confirmation
  • Guest-specific link controls
  • Authorized end-to-end test
  • Backup and replacement process
