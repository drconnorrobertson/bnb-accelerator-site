Document a Guest’s Lost-Item Return

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Airbnb advises guests who leave an item behind to message the host and explains that shipping costs can be handled through its Resolution Center. An operator still needs a practical process for identifying the item and agreeing on a return. Keep the guest’s privacy and the relevant local requirements in mind; this guide does not establish a legal retention period.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Record a found item carefully

Note where and when the item was found and which authorized person secured it. Describe it neutrally without opening private contents unnecessarily. Do not post identifying photographs publicly to find an owner. If the item presents a safety concern or appears subject to special handling rules, use the appropriate professional or authority rather than an ordinary return procedure.

Confirm the correct guest

Use the reservation’s established communication channel and enough descriptive detail to resolve ownership. Avoid sharing one guest’s belongings or information with another. A new email claiming an item should not automatically receive personal details from the booking. When uncertainty remains, ask the platform for guidance through the authorized support process.

Agree on a return method

Discuss the guest’s preferred method and confirm whether the operator can reasonably arrange it. Record any address through an appropriate private channel and check it before shipping. Do not promise a delivery date that depends on a carrier. Fragile, regulated or high-value items may need additional advice or a different handling arrangement.

Handle costs through an approved process

Airbnb’s guidance identifies the Resolution Center as a way for a guest to cover shipping. Use the current platform process where applicable and agree on the amount before incurring a disputed charge. Keep the carrier receipt and avoid adding unexplained fees. A payment request is separate from confirmation that an item has been dispatched.

Keep the record proportionate

Retain only the information needed for the return and any applicable record obligations. Limit access to the guest’s address and item description. Record tracking information and the status without copying unrelated booking documents into a general lost-property spreadsheet. Ask qualified advisers about retention duties if the situation requires a legal answer.

Use a simple return example

Suppose a guest reports a jacket after checkout. The operator matches the description through the reservation thread, confirms shipping details and cost, and retains the dispatch receipt. The record should show pending, dispatched or otherwise unresolved status accurately. Do not mark an item delivered merely because it has been placed in a package. If shipping cannot be arranged as proposed, tell the guest clearly and discuss an alternative through the established channel. Keep the item’s status accurate while the parties decide what should happen next.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Minimal found-item record
  • Ownership confirmed through trusted channel
  • Agreed return method and address
  • Approved shipping-cost arrangement
  • Dispatch evidence and limited retention
