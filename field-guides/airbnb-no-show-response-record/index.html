Airbnb Guest No-Show: Build a Response Record

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

An empty property after the expected arrival time does not by itself establish that an Airbnb guest has canceled. The guest may be delayed or unable to use a phone while traveling. Airbnb advises hosts to contact the guest and report an unresponsive no-show to support for next steps. Use a concise record so the next operator can see what is known without guessing from scattered messages.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Confirm which arrival is being discussed

Record the reservation reference, property, scheduled check-in and the arrival information actually supplied by the guest. Check the current reservation status in the account. Distinguish an expected arrival estimate from a contractual or platform deadline. Do not mark a booking canceled, reopen dates or promise a refund merely because an informal arrival time has passed.

Preserve the guest-facing instructions

Check that the guest received the correct address and current access information through the established channel. Record any known delivery problem or recent instruction change. The purpose is to identify a possible communication failure, not to infer the guest’s location from surveillance. Keep access credentials out of broadly shared incident notes and limit the record to necessary operational facts.

Document reasonable contact attempts

Use the reservation’s Airbnb message thread and the available contact options described by the platform. Note the time, channel and whether a response arrived. Keep wording factual and helpful: ask whether travel plans changed and whether arrival assistance is needed. Avoid repeated pressure, accusations or claims that nonresponse automatically forfeits every right under the booking.

Escalate with a compact case summary

If the guest remains unresponsive, contact Airbnb using the reservation and follow its instructions. Supply the timeline and current status rather than asking support to reconstruct the issue from unrelated screenshots. Record the case reference and the action actually authorized. An open support case is not the same as a completed cancellation or approval to charge another fee.

Work through a delayed-flight example

Imagine a guest expected at dinner time sends a late message that a connection was missed. Update the arrival plan and confirm access for the revised time if the stay continues. Keep the original reservation and later message linked. If the guest instead asks to change or cancel, use the applicable platform process rather than overwriting the no-show record with an assumed outcome.

Close the exception deliberately

Save the final status, guest response and any support decision. Tell the next shift whether the reservation remains active and which tasks still need attention. Review whether the original instructions caused avoidable confusion, but do not turn one travel delay into a blanket new guest restriction. The completed record should distinguish observations, communications and final decisions.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Reservation reference and current status
  • Scheduled and guest-stated arrival times
  • Instruction delivery check
  • Dated contact attempts
  • Support case and authorized next step
  • Final arrival or cancellation outcome
