Handle a Reservation Booked for Another Guest

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Airbnb’s current guidance permits authorized bookings for another person and distinguishes the booker from the staying guest. An operator should use the actual reservation information and current policy rather than assuming every such booking is prohibited. Clear roles matter for arrival communication, changes and support. Do not infer that the payer and the person arriving must always be the same individual.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Read the reservation roles

Identify the named staying guest and the person who made the booking using information available through the platform. Keep those roles distinct in the operating notes. A familiar message sender may be arranging the stay rather than occupying the property. If the roles are unclear, ask through the reservation thread or the platform’s support process.

Use the current authorization model

Airbnb describes booking permissions that allow a guest to authorize another person to book for them. Its Airbnb for Work process also supports designated travel arrangements. Consult the relevant current guidance for the reservation rather than applying an outdated blanket rule. Do not attempt to verify authorization by collecting unnecessary private identity documents.

Clarify who needs arrival information

Make sure the staying guest can access the appropriate trip and arrival details through the permitted communication arrangements. Avoid posting door codes publicly or sending them to an unverified new contact. If someone asks to add a participant, use the platform’s authorized invitation or support process and the property’s documented access controls.

Respect different reservation permissions

The official guidance describes different actions available to the booker and guest. Before processing a requested alteration, review who is entitled to make that request under the applicable workflow. Do not treat every participant’s message as authority to change the reservation. Keep disputed or unclear instructions pending while obtaining the proper clarification.

Keep guest treatment consistent

A booking arranged by another person does not justify assumptions about the guest’s reliability or purpose. Apply accurate, consistent house rules and current platform policies. Where a question involves local legal requirements, obtain qualified advice separately. The platform’s booking features should not be presented as a determination of those requirements.

Use a family-booking example

Suppose a relative books an authorized stay for a traveler and participates in the thread. The operator can record the booker and staying guest separately, check that the traveler receives arrival information and direct change requests through the correct workflow. That is more useful than either ignoring the distinction or rejecting the arrangement based on an obsolete rule. A contact handoff should explain who is receiving each message, not merely list several names. If the staying guest changes, resolve that through the platform’s current process before treating an informal introduction as a confirmed reservation update.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Named booker and staying guest
  • Applicable authorization workflow
  • Secure arrival communication
  • Correct change-request authority
  • Consistent house-rule application
