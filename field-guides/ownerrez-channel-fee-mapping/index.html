Test OwnerRez Channel Rate and Fee Mapping

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

OwnerRez documents differences in the rates and fees supported by its channel integrations. A connected channel does not necessarily interpret every charge in the same way. Build a small test plan around the actual properties and fee structure you intend to use. This guide describes an evaluation workflow; it does not certify a configuration or determine tax treatment.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Inventory the proposed charges

List the nightly rate, discounts, cleaning or other surcharges, and any relevant tax settings. Identify the source and purpose of each line. Separate charges paid by the guest from platform or processor costs borne by the operator. A headline nightly amount cannot explain whether the complete quote has been mapped correctly.

Review the integration-specific documentation

Read OwnerRez’s current rate-and-fee differences for each connected channel. Check which fields transfer and what setup is required. Do not assume that a configuration tested on one channel behaves identically elsewhere. Mark any unsupported or unclear item for vendor support rather than forcing it into an unrelated field.

Choose a repeatable quote scenario

Use the same dates, guest count and stay length when comparing the intended result. Follow the vendor’s permitted testing tools and avoid creating fake paid bookings. Keep a record of the inputs and displayed totals. If the scenario changes midway through the test, restart the comparison instead of treating different quotes as equivalent.

Reconcile the difference by line item

Compare the base rate and each applicable charge before comparing totals. Identify whether a discrepancy comes from inclusion, calculation basis, rounding or a separate channel charge. Do not simply adjust the nightly rate until the final figure looks close. That can hide a mapping problem that reappears for another stay length.

Keep tax and contract questions with advisers

Software documentation explains technical behavior; it does not decide the legal tax treatment of a charge or the owner’s contractual allocation. Give the relevant facts to the appropriate professional. Preserve the approved configuration and the reasoning behind it so the operator does not later reverse a deliberate setting without review.

Retest a material change

Suppose a new optional surcharge is added after launch. Review its mapping for every affected channel and repeat the controlled quote test. Record the configuration version and results. A previously correct setup is not evidence that a newly introduced fee will be handled correctly without checking it. Keep the test result tied to the configuration version, channel and date. When a channel changes its fee support, the old result becomes a historical reference rather than current proof. Assign someone to review the vendor’s relevant update and decide whether the affected quote scenarios need to be checked again before new bookings use the configuration.
