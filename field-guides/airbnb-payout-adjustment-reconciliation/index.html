Reconcile an Airbnb Payout Adjustment

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

An Airbnb payout can differ from the amount expected for the current stay because an adjustment relates to another reservation. Airbnb describes adjustments arising from cancellations, reservation changes or refund-policy issues, with amounts deducted from later payouts. Build a transaction trail before classifying the difference as a missing payment. The worksheet is an operational reconciliation aid, not a determination of tax treatment.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Capture the original adjustment

Open the earnings record in the authenticated account and identify the adjustment reference, amount, currency and connected reservation. Save the platform’s description and effective date. Do not rely only on the bank deposit, which may combine several booking amounts. Keep original records when downloading a revised report so the change can be explained later.

Separate source event from collection

Record why the adjustment arose and when Airbnb collected it through later payouts. Those may occur in different periods. A booking cancellation record and a later deduction can describe the same financial event, so avoid posting both as unrelated expenses. Give the bookkeeper the connected references and let the agreed accounting process determine the correct entries.

Review routing and shared payouts

Airbnb explains that payout routing and co-host arrangements can affect how an adjustment is distributed. Identify the settings applicable to the affected booking and the later payout. Do not assume the owner’s bank account bears the entire amount. Ask the relevant parties for their records before requesting a separate reimbursement that might duplicate a platform deduction.

Build a worked reconciliation

For an illustrative case, a prior stay has an adjustment and a new stay produces an otherwise expected payout. List the new booking amount, the separately identified prior adjustment and the resulting transfer. Match the transfer to the bank entry by reference and currency. The example establishes a method; use actual platform figures rather than a made-up percentage rule.

Route a disagreement accurately

If the reason or amount is unclear, contact support with the linked reservation and adjustment references. Distinguish a disagreement with the original decision from a problem tracing collection. Preserve the support response and any revision. Do not change account or payment settings simply to test whether the deduction disappears, and do not assume a pending case suspends collection.

Finish with an exception balance

Record any remaining amount and the next payout in which it appears. Give the owner and bookkeeper one reconciled summary with supporting files. If the account offers a way to pay an outstanding adjustment directly, review its eligibility and terms before acting. A cleared balance closes this reconciliation task without proving how every underlying dispute was resolved.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Adjustment and reservation references
  • Original event and collection dates
  • Amount and currency
  • Routing and co-host impact
  • Bank-transfer match
  • Remaining balance and support outcome
