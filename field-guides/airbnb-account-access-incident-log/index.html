Create an Airbnb Account-Access Incident Log

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

If an Airbnb account may have been accessed without permission, use Airbnb’s official account-review process promptly. An incident log supports recovery by separating confirmed changes from suspicions and identifying affected operating records. It should not delay securing the account or contacting the platform. Do not copy passwords, authentication codes or full financial details into the log.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Use the official review route

Navigate directly to Airbnb through a trusted bookmark or typed address and follow its account-review instructions. Avoid links supplied in a suspicious message. Airbnb says the process may include changing the password and reviewing recent logins. If a password was reused elsewhere, address that exposure through the relevant service’s official security process as well.

Record the first known facts

Note when the issue was discovered, what appeared unusual and which legitimate user noticed it. Separate an unfamiliar login location from a confirmed unauthorized action. Save relevant notifications without spreading sensitive account data. Assign a recovery owner and a backup so multiple staff members do not make conflicting changes while trying to help.

Review operational changes

Airbnb specifically identifies contact information, profile photos, listings, payout methods and reservations as items to inspect. Track each category as reviewed, changed or unresolved. A restored password does not establish that every booking or payout setting is correct. Compare suspicious changes with known authorized work before reversing them.

Use a payout-change example

Suppose the owner sees an unfamiliar payout method and a listing description edit. Follow the official recovery process and report the changes with their visible dates. Review affected reservations and payments with support. Do not send a test payout or transfer money to determine who controls the unfamiliar account, and do not publish its full details in staff chat.

Coordinate guest communication

If a guest received incorrect instructions, use a verified channel after securing access and coordinate with support. State the corrected operational information clearly without asking the guest to forward passwords or payment credentials. Preserve the message history needed for the case. Avoid mass messages speculating about the incident before the affected reservations are identified.

Close only after reviewing the effects

Record the support case, restored settings and remaining actions. Confirm legitimate team access and remove unneeded access through the supported controls. Store the incident summary securely with a review date. A completed log documents the response; it does not prove that every external account or device is secure or that no financial issue remains. Ask each authorized team member to confirm their own recent work through a trusted channel. That can help distinguish a legitimate update from an unexplained change without distributing account credentials.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Discovery time and observed change
  • Official recovery action and case reference
  • Listing/contact/reservation review
  • Payout-method review status
  • Affected guest corrections
  • Remaining actions and access review
