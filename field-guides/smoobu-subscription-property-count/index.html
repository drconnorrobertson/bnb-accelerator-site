Compare Smoobu Subscription Basis and Property Counts

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Smoobu’s official plan documentation describes different subscription structures, including a prepaid model and a flexible option with booking-based charges. The appropriate comparison depends on the actual properties, users and billing term. Do not use an old review’s starting price as a complete estimate for a different portfolio or current account.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Define the bookable units

List the properties and any parent or sub-unit arrangement you plan to configure. Ask Smoobu how that setup is counted for the proposed subscription. A physical building, a listing and a bookable unit may not be interchangeable. Resolve the count before comparing plans rather than assuming the most favorable interpretation.

Compare the cost structure

Review the current official descriptions of the prepaid, flexible and team-oriented options. Distinguish fixed subscription charges from booking-related fees. Use the same operating assumptions for each comparison and record the billing term and currency. A lower fixed amount may not mean a lower total cost under every booking pattern.

Include access and add-ons

Identify who needs viewing access and who needs to edit the system. The plan documentation distinguishes access and additional charges. List other tools you intend to enable rather than assuming they are included. Ask for clarification where the website and account offer differ, and retain the written answer with the subscription decision.

Model seasonality transparently

Compare an ordinary operating period with a quieter one using the vendor’s current terms. Keep the property count and booking assumptions visible. Do not convert a promotional period into a permanent monthly estimate. Where a fee depends on bookings, confirm which bookings and amounts are included in its calculation.

Check renewal and cancellation terms

Read the terms for the actual billing cycle before committing. Determine when a requested change takes effect and what happens to prepaid amounts or separately enabled products. Disabling a channel connection is not the same as canceling the software subscription. Record the relevant account process and responsibility for reviewing renewal notices.

Use a team-growth example

Suppose a host adds a second operator who needs editing access. Recalculate the proposed configuration using current access charges and plan rules. Compare that result with a team-oriented plan if appropriate. Keep the decision tied to required permissions and total terms rather than assuming that more properties are the only reason the price can change. Check the actual invoice after the first billing period against the documented configuration. If the result differs, identify whether the difference concerns property count, users, tax, booking charges or the selected term. Ask support to explain the discrepancy with that evidence rather than treating a different total as proof that the public pricing was inaccurate.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Confirmed unit-counting basis
  • Fixed and booking-based charges separated
  • User permissions and add-ons
  • Consistent seasonal assumptions
  • Renewal and change terms
