Track a Vrbo Damage-Deposit Release

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Vrbo describes several different arrangements: a card on file, an upfront refundable damage deposit and a property-damage protection product. They should not be recorded as though they are the same payment. Before answering a guest’s deposit question, identify what the specific booking used and review the current platform status. This worksheet organizes evidence without deciding a disputed damage claim.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Identify the booked arrangement

Save the relevant booking terms and payment breakdown. Confirm whether money was actually collected as a refundable deposit or whether the arrangement was a card on file or a separate protection product. Vrbo explains that card details are not shared with the host in its card-on-file arrangement. Do not ask the guest to send full card information to reconstruct the record.

Record the applicable dates

Use the actual checkout date and the current platform guidance to identify the relevant review or release window. Vrbo’s deposit guidance describes a 14-day process in the situations covered, with additional financial-institution processing possible. Check the specific account and booking terms rather than promising the guest that money will appear on a particular bank date.

Separate a claim from a refund question

If a damage claim exists, record its reference, amount, submitted evidence and status through the appropriate process. If no claim exists, focus on the release record. Do not create a damage allegation merely because a guest asks about timing. Keep any assessment factual and distinguish ordinary cleaning, wear and disputed damage according to the applicable rules.

Use a currency example

Suppose a guest sees a different home-currency amount when the deposit returns. Compare the original charged currency and refund currency before treating the difference as withheld money. Vrbo notes that exchange rates and bank fees can affect converted amounts. Ask for a redacted transaction reference if needed, not a full bank statement containing unrelated personal information.

Route an unresolved release

Check whether the platform shows release, pending processing or a claim-related hold. Contact the appropriate support channel with the reservation and transaction references when the status is unclear. Avoid sending a separate manual refund until the original transaction is traced, because duplicate refunds can create a second dispute and obscure the accounting record.

Close with a reconciled outcome

Record the final platform status and any guest confirmation available. Keep deposit movements separate from rental income in the operational file, leaving accounting classification to the responsible professional. Retain the relevant evidence and remove unnecessary financial data. The record should explain what happened, who handled it and which questions remain unresolved. Tell the next shift which party is expected to act next, so the same guest does not receive conflicting explanations of the release status.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Booked deposit/protection arrangement
  • Checkout and relevant review dates
  • Claim reference or no-claim status
  • Release transaction and currency
  • Support case and bank-processing distinction
  • Final reconciled outcome
