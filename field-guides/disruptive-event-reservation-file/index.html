Prepare a Reservation File for a Major Disruptive Event

AI-assisted editorial guide · Published November 20, 2026

Airbnb’s Major Disruptive Events Policy addresses certain large-scale events affecting reservations. The platform assesses whether the policy applies. An operator should preserve accurate facts and use current official information rather than promising a refund or fee waiver from a news headline. Immediate safety instructions should come from the relevant public authorities.

Decision processChecklistOfficial sources

Identify the affected reservation facts

Record the listing location, stay dates and the specific event being discussed. Separate an event at the destination from an unrelated disruption elsewhere. Use the actual reservation rather than a general description of regional bookings. Keep guest information limited to what the support request needs and avoid posting reservation details publicly.

Collect authoritative event information

Save relevant official notices with their issue dates and geographic scope. A social post or news summary may omit important boundaries or updates. Follow the appropriate authority for current safety directions. This article does not assess whether travel or occupancy is safe and should not replace an evacuation order or other official instruction.

Read the current platform policy

Review Airbnb’s Major Disruptive Events Policy and its stated scope before making representations to guests. Different cancellation or refund processes may apply to different circumstances. Do not infer that every weather event qualifies or that a policy used for an earlier incident automatically applies to the current one.

Contact support with a concise record

Explain the reservation, event and unresolved question, and provide relevant official evidence through the approved channel. Ask what process applies rather than declaring the outcome yourself. Preserve the response and case reference. A support enquiry is not the same as an approved cancellation or confirmed adjustment.

Coordinate guest and vendor communications

Give guests accurate status updates without promising a result that has not been confirmed. Keep the operating team informed about affected arrivals and service work. Avoid sending a normal arrival message when the stay is under active review. Assign one person to consolidate updates so multiple staff members do not provide conflicting statements.

Reconcile the outcome after a decision

When the platform confirms an action, check the reservation, payout and downstream tasks against that decision. Record any remaining discrepancy for follow-up. For example, a canceled arrival may still have a scheduled cleaner or automated message. Resolve those practical items while keeping the official policy decision distinct from the owner’s internal operating changes. Give every update an observation time so the team can distinguish a superseded notice from the current instruction. When official boundaries change, revisit affected reservations instead of extending an earlier conclusion to a larger area.

Prepare the evidence and next action

  • Reservation location and dates
  • Dated official notices
  • Current policy scope
  • Support decision and reference
  • Guest, payout and vendor updates
