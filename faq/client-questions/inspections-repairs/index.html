STR Inspections and Repair Negotiations

Plan inspections, specialist reviews, repair estimates, seller negotiations and reinspections before buying an STR.

Practical answers for buying and launching a short-term rental with BNB Accelerator. Updated September 30, 2026.

All client questionsRead the related guideBook a Call
What inspections should I order?

The TC team will tell you what inspections the home needs, it varies per house.

Who schedules and pays for inspections?

The TC team schedules it, you pay for it.

When do I need a structural engineer vs. contractor?

Structural engineers are specialists that will check structural issues with a home, whereas contractors will be for smaller non structural issues.

Does the inspector provide repair estimates?

A standard home inspection primarily identifies condition issues and may not include reliable repair pricing. Ask the inspector what is included, then obtain written quotes from appropriately qualified contractors or specialists for material issues.

How do we decide what repairs are worth negotiating?

We always request the most expensive items AND safety items. Then the rest is dependent on you as a buyer! These repairs are typically a small part in larger negotiation picture.

Seller repair vs. seller credit , which is better?

A lender-approved credit or price reduction can give you control over the repair after closing. Seller-completed repairs may be necessary for safety, financing, insurance or rental readiness.

Compare repair quality, timing, available cash and lender limits. If the seller performs work, document the required scope, permits and completion evidence, and arrange a reinspection where appropriate.

What happens if the seller refuses our repair/credit request?

Then you have to decide if the home is worth pursuing or not given the offer as it stands. Typically an unhappy buyer will terminate the deal unless the sellers meets their minimum request.

What is a reinspection?

A reinspection happens once the seller does repairs to a home. OR if we need to send a specialist to check on an issue from the original inspection report.

Can we extend the inspection deadline?

Yes, if the seller and buyer both agree and sign an addendum the deadline can be extended.
