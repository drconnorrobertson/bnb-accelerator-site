How to research Big Bear Lake

Start with date-level comparable listings, not annual averages. Map snow weekends, summer lake trips, and holiday demand, then sample ordinary weekdays and the softest visible period. Verify local requirements directly with the city, county, state tax authority, HOA, insurer, and lender. Save URLs and the verification date.

Local thesis: Big Bear Lake is driven by mountain lake; the likely guest is Southern California drive-to groups; the formats most worth testing are cabins with gathering space. Those are hypotheses to validate, not promises.
