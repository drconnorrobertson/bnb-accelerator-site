The Three Pillars

Cash flow, tax savings, and none of the work

Most high earners do not have a money problem. They have a time problem and a tax problem. The Reverse Offset Method™ solves both with a single asset.

Cash Flow

We only buy properties that pencil. Every deal is underwritten against real booking data, not listing-agent optimism. If the numbers do not clear our threshold after management, cleaning, taxes, and debt service, we kill it and move on.

Tax Savings

Short-term rentals sit outside the passive activity rules when you meet the seven-day test and materially participate. Paired with a cost segregation study, that can turn a single purchase into a six-figure deduction against your W-2 or business income.

The Reverse Offset Method™

Stop writing $150K checks to the IRS

If you earn $500,000 or more, you are likely sending six figures to the federal government every April with nothing to show for it.

The Reverse Offset Method™ flips that. Instead of paying tax on income and then trying to invest what is left, you deploy capital into a short-term rental first, accelerate the depreciation, and use the resulting paper loss to offset the income you already earned.

  • Buy an asset that produces monthly cash flow
  • Meet the seven-day average stay test so it is not passive
  • Materially participate under one of the IRS tests
  • Run a cost segregation study to front-load depreciation
  • Apply the loss against W-2 and business income

FAQ

Common questions

What does BNB Accelerator actually do?

We are a done-for-you short-term rental acquisition firm, not a course or a coaching program. We screen more than 1,000 listings a week across eight states, underwrite the survivors against real booking data, write and negotiate the offer, coordinate inspection, appraisal, lender and title, and pair you with vetted local designers and property managers. You review, you decide, you sign. See the full process.

How much money do I need to start short term rental investing in 2026?

For a financed purchase, plan on 20 to 25 percent down plus furnishing, closing costs, and operating reserves. On a $700,000 property that is typically $175,000 to $220,000 all in. Second home financing can lower the down payment where the property genuinely qualifies, and DSCR loans qualify on property income rather than your personal income. See short term rental financing.

How does a short-term rental reduce my taxes?

A rental with an average stay of seven days or less is not a passive activity under the Section 469 rules. If you also materially participate, a loss created by accelerated depreciation can offset W-2 and business income in the same year. A cost segregation study is what makes that loss large enough to matter. See the tax strategy and STR tax benefits and cost segregation. We are not a CPA firm and this is not tax advice.

How long does the process take?

About 45 days from strategy call to a live, revenue-producing listing, because sourcing, financing, furnishing, and management setup run in parallel rather than one after another. Buyers doing this alone typically take three to six months, and most of that time goes to sourcing properties that do not pencil.

Which markets do you buy in?

Eight states: Florida, Tennessee, Arizona, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Texas, Colorado, and Missouri. We buy where regulation is stable, demand is proven, and the revenue-to-price ratio still supports cash flow after debt service. See the market data.
