Your Questions — Answered

How Our System Works

What do you actually do?

We help define your property criteria, source and evaluate deals, negotiate terms and coordinate the transaction. After closing, we introduce local designers and property managers to support the launch. See the acquisition process.

How does the tax strategy work?

Short-term rental ownership may offer tax benefits depending on your circumstances. We introduce independent CPAs and cost segregation providers; your advisers determine eligibility and the strategy appropriate for you. Explore tax strategy resources.

What about material participation?

Your participation and recordkeeping responsibilities matter. A hands-off acquisition service does not automatically satisfy tax requirements. Discuss the applicable tests with your CPA. Learn about material participation.

Who designs and manages the home?

We pair you with local designers and property managers. You review vendor scope, fees and agreements before proceeding. Learn about the management handoff.

How do I evaluate the risk and budget?

Review the full acquisition cost, reserves, financing, regulations and a downside scenario before buying. Build your purchase budget. Review service pricing.
