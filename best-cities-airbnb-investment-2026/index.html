What makes a market worth investing in

Not every city that performs well on Airbnb is a good investment market. The markets worth investing in have strong, diverse demand drivers, a stable regulatory environment, acquisition costs that support positive cash flow, and enough operational infrastructure to run properties at a professional level.

The markets BNB Accelerator is buying in

Smoky Mountains (Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge, TN). More than 12 million annual visitors. Genuine four-season demand. Cabin inventory where the property itself is the attraction. ADR: $175-$290. Occupancy: 67-77%.

Gulf Coast (Gulf Shores, AL and Destin, FL). Beachfront inventory at prices below comparable Florida markets. Long season March through October. ADR: $155-$280. Occupancy: 65-76%.

Scottsdale and Greater Phoenix, AZ. Spring training, golf, corporate travel, and event venues. Premium rates with diverse demand. ADR: $210-$320. Occupancy: 68-78%.

Savannah, GA. Walkability market with genuine rate premium. Year-round tourism. ADR: $165-$250. Occupancy: 70-79%.

Asheville, NC. Craft beer and culinary tourism. Blue Ridge Parkway access. ADR: $160-$240. Occupancy: 69-78%.

Charleston, SC. Historic charm, culinary tourism, beach proximity. ADR: $175-$270. Occupancy: 71-80%.

Joshua Tree, CA. Design-driven market. Professional furnishing creates the widest performance gap. ADR: $195-$340. Occupancy: 60-72%.

Broken Bow, OK. Luxury cabin market serving the DFW drive market. ADR: $180-$300. Occupancy: 62-73%.

Why market selection should not be your job

Choosing the right market requires analyzing regulatory trajectory, demand diversity, supply growth, seasonal patterns, and submarket dynamics at a level most individual investors cannot replicate.
