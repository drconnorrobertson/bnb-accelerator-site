STR Tax Savings Checklist

A plain-English checklist of every condition that has to hold for a short-term rental loss to offset your W-2 income, written so you can walk through it with your CPA rather than hope it works out. Covers the seven day test, material participation evidence, management structure, and cost segregation timing.

What the checklist covers

  1. The seven day average stay test. How it is calculated across the year, and the booking patterns that quietly break it.
  2. Material participation. The three usable tests, what hours count, and what a log that survives scrutiny actually looks like.
  3. Management structure. Which arrangements preserve the position and which eliminate it, and why the decision belongs before closing.
  4. Cost segregation timing. Placed-in-service dates, look-back studies, and the questions to ask a provider.
  5. The limits. Excess business loss, state conformity to bonus depreciation, and recapture on exit.
  6. Year-end items that routinely get missed and cannot be fixed in April.

My BnB Accelerator, LLC is a real estate acquisition firm, not a CPA firm. This checklist explains mechanics so you can ask better questions of a qualified professional. It is not tax advice. See our partner firm.
