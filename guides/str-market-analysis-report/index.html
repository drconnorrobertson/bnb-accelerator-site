Free Short-Term Rental Market Analysis

Tell us the market you are considering and we will build a written analysis: comparable revenue and occupancy for your property profile, the seasonality shape and what it means for reserves, the current regulatory picture, and an honest read on whether it clears our underwriting.

What you get

  1. A comparable set, named. The eight to twelve active listings we used, so you can check the work rather than trust it.
  2. Estimated ADR and occupancy for the property profile you describe, reported as a range around the median rather than a single flattering number.
  3. Seasonality shape month by month, and the reserve the trough implies.
  4. The regulatory picture as it currently stands, including whether permits are capped and whether the trend is tightening.
  5. An honest verdict. If we would not buy there, we say so and explain why.

What it is not

It is not a projection for a specific address, and it is not an appraisal. Market-level analysis narrows the search. Only underwriting an individual property against its own comparables tells you whether to buy it.
