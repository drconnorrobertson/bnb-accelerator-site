How Much Cash Do You Need to Buy a Short-Term Rental?

Updated September 2026·4 min read

The down payment is only one part of the cash needed to buy an Airbnb. A buyer can qualify for a loan and still run short during furnishing or the first off-season. Build a sources-and-uses schedule before you make an offer.

List every use of cash

Start with earnest money and down payment, then lender and title charges, inspections, appraisal, insurance deposit, prepaid tax and interest, and any immediate repair or code work. Add furniture, mattresses, kitchen inventory, linens, exterior amenities, photography, listing setup, permits, and sales or lodging tax registration where required.

Add working capital for utility deposits, early mortgage payments, vendor retainers, and a revenue ramp. Keep an emergency reserve separate from the furnishing budget; spending every available dollar to launch leaves no buffer for a failed HVAC unit or a weak first quarter.

Illustrative math

For a $600,000 property with 25% down, the down payment is $150,000. If closing and prepaids are $18,000, repairs and furnishing are $65,000, launch costs are $7,000, and operating reserves are $30,000, total planned cash is $270,000. Those figures are an arithmetic example, not a quote or a typical deal.

A seller credit may reduce closing cash but cannot fund every category. Financing products differ in what they allow you to roll into the loan. Confirm actual terms with a lender and build the model from written bids.

Match the reserve to the risk

A property with a hot tub, pool, remote location, or concentrated seasonal demand may need more liquidity than a simple urban unit. Recalculate cash needs if insurance, interest rate, or renovation scope changes. Our closing cost and reserve guide expands the financing side.

Frequently asked questions

Is the down payment enough to start an Airbnb?

Usually no. Buyers also need closing funds, setup and furnishing money, permits, early operating cash, and reserves for repairs or slow months.

Can I finance STR furniture and setup costs?

Some products may allow financing for certain improvements, but terms vary. Assume cash is required until your lender confirms the allowable uses in writing.
